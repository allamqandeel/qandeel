## Cross-source synthesis

1. All sources agree motion must be interruptible at every frame and that new input **retargets** a
   running animation with velocity continuity; cancel-and-restart is the anti-pattern.
2. All agree on gesture coupling: 1:1 while the finger is down, physics only after release, seeded
   with the tracked velocity.
3. All agree on continuity from origin to destination and forbid a 0 ms replacement.
4. All agree the most frequent interactions get the quietest motion and hero motion is reserved.
5. Apple alone specifies reduced motion concretely (fades for x/y/z travel, tightened springs,
   gesture tracking kept, no blur, no depth) — that list is the shootout's reduced-motion spec.
6. Disagreement on default character: Material Expressive overshoots by default; Apple asks for
   brevity and precision; Compose's own `spring()` default is no-bounce. QANDEEL's P6 sides with
   the no-bounce default; Direction C tests how much overshoot survives a long session.
7. Disagreement on parameterization: Material publishes a token grid; Apple publishes almost no
   numbers and treats duration as emergent from the spring. The lab keeps every number a hypothesis.

## Repository-specific notes

- The lab uses Reanimated 4's duration/dampingRatio spring form throughout (one designer pair),
  never mass/stiffness, so a human can read every value.
- `runOnJS` is not used anywhere; every UI→RN hop is `scheduleOnRN` at a boundary (rest, tap,
  report), never per frame.
