# 1. Post-review amendment / closure clarification

The independent adversarial review accepted Phase V with one conditional semantic finding.
`V10-FIX-01` discharges it. Nine statements close the phase.

1. **Phase V remains structurally sound.** The independent review did not overturn the
   grammar, the evidence or the freeze verdict. Nothing in the freeze is withdrawn.

2. **`V10-FIX-01` corrects exactly one over-broad semantic prohibition.** The freeze's
   near/far language read as an absolute ban on QANDEEL ever repositioning Live Context
   items by their relationship to the current conversation. That wording was written to
   block a *rank*; as written it also deleted an intended future QANDEEL capability that is
   not a rank. The prohibition on ordinal near/far survives intact and unweakened; only its
   over-reach is scoped. This is a wording repair, not a design change.

3. **User-driven foreground remains reversible and non-semantic.** A reader bringing
   something forward means only «I am looking at this now». It is initiated by the reader,
   reversible, local to inspection, and carries no judgement by the system: not a rank, not
   a score, not confidence, not importance, not truth, not evidence strength, not priority.
   It never silently mutates canonical product state. Freeze item **A9**, unweakened.

4. **Live Context contextual-relevance spatialization is a `RESERVED FUTURE CAPABILITY`.**
   Inside Live Context, a future runtime-backed system may position or recompose items
   nearer/farther or more/less present **solely** to express how related an item is to what
   the current conversation is about right now. «Nearer / more present» may mean **only**
   *more contextually related to the current conversation*. Freeze item **A22**.

5. **It is `SIMULATED ONLY` until a real runtime contract exists.** The capability may be
   simulated in later design work to reach Live Context's final visual form, and every such
   fixture must be declared **SIMULATED FOR EXPERIENCE/DESIGN TESTING**. A simulation is
   never evidence of production support and must never be presented as one.

6. **Current production behaviour remains `NOT SUPPORTED`.** No client-readable relevance
   computation suitable for this spatial behaviour exists. Production's bounded material
   selection is a recency window, and recency is not a contract for contextual-relevance
   spatialization. Production UI must not claim or visually imply that any current near/far
   position is runtime-derived contextual relevance. Ordinary composition and responsive
   layout differences may exist; they may never masquerade as computed relevance. The full
   gate — current, prototype and future production — is in the freeze's
   "Runtime / Truth Boundaries".

7. **No visual channel may convert relevance into confidence, rank, truth, strength,
   importance or priority.** The non-equivalence law is now explicit in the freeze:
   **contextual relevance ≠ importance ≠ truth ≠ confidence ≠ evidence strength ≠ priority
   ≠ rank ≠ certainty ≠ correctness.** Contextual relevance answers only *"how related is
   this item to what the conversation is about right now?"* It does not answer how
   important, how true, how strongly supported, how confident QANDEEL is, what to
   prioritise, which reading wins or which item is correct. Proximity, size, depth,
   position, motion, contrast and persistence remain barred from encoding any of those
   (freeze item **A15**), and the reserved exception grants proximity one meaning inside one
   surface — it does not open a generic visual weighting channel, does not reach peer
   readings or Deep Analysis, leaves peer equality untouched, and is not a numerical product
   metric. Its meaning is direction-independent: nearer and farther mean the same thing in
   Arabic and English, RTL and LTR, and no directional convention may imply rank. The
   reserved capability also may not be the sole carrier of its own meaning: it requires a
   non-spatial accessible equivalent and must stay understandable under reduced motion
   (freeze item **A23**).

8. **No Phase V exploration is reopened.** V5B, V6, V7, V8, V9, Matn, Threshold and Wizn
   stay closed. No new direction, prototype, screenshot, interaction model, colour,
   typography, surface, iconography, motion language or brand work was produced or implied
   by this amendment. Rejected directions stay rejected — `D7` still rejects rank-like
   near/far permanently; what changed is that relatedness-to-context is recorded as
   *reserved* rather than swept into that rejection.

9. **Phase V is CLOSED finally.** With `V10-FIX-01` verified, the phase is closed. Its
   remaining work is the archival checkpoint, not another round.

# 2. Carried-forward open items — re-prioritised

The Final Visual Design phase inherits the freeze's "Open for Final Visual Design" list.
`V10-FIX-01` re-prioritises it so the phase begins with the item the independent review
raised, and changes nothing else about it.

## #1 HIGH-PRIORITY OPEN ITEM — Large / double-digit peer navigation

The current mobile pager has a measured practical ceiling of **six controls** at 375px;
at seven, labels wrap and clip. The runtime caps nothing and can expose peer counts far
above that ceiling — into double digits.

The closure is explicit about what the solution may not do:

- **no peer may be silently omitted;**
- **no peer may be silently truncated away because the peer count grows;**
- **all peers must remain reachable, at any peer count;**
- the current six-control pager is **prototype evidence** of where the present form breaks —
  it is not the final solution and must not be read as one;
- the final visual and navigation solution is **deferred to Phase VI**.

This is a real product-navigation question, not a styling one. It is **not solved here**,
and no pager redesign is proposed, sketched or implied by this document.

The remaining open items keep their existing content and follow in the order recorded in
the freeze.
