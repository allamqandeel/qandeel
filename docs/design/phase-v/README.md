## `V10-FIX-01` clarification — Contextual Relevance

- Contextual-relevance spatialization inside Live Context is a
  **`RESERVED FUTURE CAPABILITY`**. It is reserved, not rejected, and not forbidden.
- It is **NOT current production behavior**, and no current near/far position may be
  claimed or visually implied to be runtime-derived contextual relevance.
- The current runtime retrieval is a **recency window, not a client-readable relevance
  computation**, and recency is not a contract for relevance spatialization.
- Future near/far may represent contextual relevance **only after a real, explicit,
  client-consumable runtime contract exists** (defined semantics, source and provenance,
  freshness, bounded failure, no aliasing to confidence/importance/rank, and an accessible
  non-spatial equivalent). It may be **simulated** in design work only when labelled
  `SIMULATED FOR EXPERIENCE/DESIGN TESTING`.
- Contextual relevance must **never** be visually conflated with importance, confidence,
  truth, evidence strength, priority, rank, certainty or correctness. "Nearer / more
  present" may mean only *more contextually related to the current conversation*.
- **User-driven foreground remains reversible and non-semantic** — the reader's own view
  state, never a system judgement, never a rank, never a mutation of stored state.

## Highest-priority open item for Phase VI

**#1 — Large / double-digit peer navigation and scalability.**

- **No peer may be silently omitted, silently truncated away, or made unreachable as peer
  counts grow.** All peers must remain reachable at any count.
- The prototype's six-control mobile pager at 375px is **measured evidence of where the
  present form breaks — it is NOT a final solution.**
- The runtime caps nothing and can expose peer counts into double digits.
- Not solved in Phase V, and deliberately so. The final visual and navigation answer
  belongs to Phase VI.

The remaining open items follow in the order recorded in the V10 Structural Grammar Freeze.
