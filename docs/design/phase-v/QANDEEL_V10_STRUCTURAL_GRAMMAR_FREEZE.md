> # This is NOT QANDEEL's final visual design or graphic language.
>
> It freezes the **rules of the language** — how understanding is organised, how screens
> relate, what relationships may be expressed, what spatial behaviour means, and what must
> remain visually non-ordinal.
>
> It does **not** freeze the final typeface, ink, paper, graphics, iconography, surfaces,
> branding, motion language, micro-interaction styling or component styling. Those belong
> to the next phase: **Final Visual Design + Design Systemization.**
>
> Every colour, type size, spacing value, radius, easing curve, measure, numeral choice and
> margin drawing in the V9 and V10 prototypes is **temporary**.

Everything below survived the V10 stress tests. Nothing is frozen because it appeared in
V9. Each item carries the reason it survived, in one line.

# LOCK — Experience / Semantic

*Meaning and product behaviour are frozen. How they look is not.*

| # | Locked | Why it survived |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | **The restraint gradient.** Conversation carries one spatial trace; the context field carries one device; Deep Analysis carries the full grammar. Depth of expression follows depth of surface | Held under density and at three peers; the conversation stayed a conversation with 11 records and three readings in the session |
| A2 | **Peer readings are equal and unranked.** No winner, no primary, no main-and-secondary, no order that means merit | The only reason the V9 defects mattered. Now asserted geometrically at any peer count, not claimed in prose |
| A3 | **No peer may be omitted, narrowed or truncated because there are many of them** | V9 dropped the third reading entirely (B1). The runtime does not cap readings, so the grammar must not |
| A4 | **A recorded item that stands in more than one reading is shown once**, with every reading it stands in named at the record, and the role it carries in each | Held with three shared records in one scene and with a record shared by two of three peers |
| A5 | **Role membership is explicit and nominal** — supporting / contradicting, stated in words, never weighted, never counted as strength | Survived the full non-ordinal audit; no ranking vocabulary appears anywhere, not even in denial |
| A6 | **Relationship semantics are nominal, never ordinal.** Kinds, not amounts | The whole point of the V9 direction, and the axis V7 found V6 weakest on. Asserted live in every state |
| A7 | **An unknown is named, explicit, and occupies space** while open; its state is RESOLVED or SUPERSEDED in words, never inferred | Held at three unknowns across two readings, and with an unknown belonging to a single peer |
| A8 | **Provenance is discoverable at the record**, one press away, without metadata overload, and states that the date is a record date and the text is not a transcription | Identical on three surfaces; carried the cross-screen continuity test |
| A9 | **Reader-driven foreground is reversible and non-semantic.** Only the reader raises anything into foreground; the surface says so while it is active; QANDEEL never foregrounds an item as a judgement about that item, and at the current production baseline QANDEEL does not reposition anything on its own. This is a rule about **foreground**, not a blanket prohibition on every future system-driven spatial behaviour — the separately-defined, runtime-gated Live Context contextual-relevance capability in **A22** is outside it and is not forbidden by it | Held at density and at five field items; raising a shared record recedes everything else, and raising anything else recedes the shared record. Scoped by `V10-FIX-01`: the original wording read as an absolute ban and deleted an intended future capability rather than the ranking risk it was written to block |
| A10 | **Spatial change never invents causal runtime behaviour.** Recomposition recomputes nothing and changes nothing stored | Asserted before and after every foreground and recomposition action |
| A11 | **Correction is RECEIVED / PENDING and nothing else.** Material, readings, roles and unknowns are identical before and after; nothing on the correction path animates | Re-verified under the dense fixture; the repeated-notice defect (B6) was the only failure and is fixed |
| A12 | **Thin data is a valid product state** — zero readings, one reading, no relation, no unknown | Carried unchanged from V9's fixtures, re-run in V10 |
| A13 | **Dense data degrades gracefully, never by hiding.** Nothing may be dropped, collapsed away or silently truncated to fit | B1 and B3 both violated this by omission; it is now an explicit lock |
| A14 | **Counts are morphology, never figures.** A real count must never be able to read as a score | Held past two readings, where V9's vocabulary ran out |
| A15 | **No visual dimension may encode confidence, rank, strength, certainty or importance.** Text disclaimers do not repair visual ranking. **The non-equivalence law: contextual relevance ≠ importance ≠ truth ≠ confidence ≠ evidence strength ≠ priority ≠ rank ≠ certainty ≠ correctness.** The single reserved exception (A22) lets proximity carry *relatedness to the current conversation* and nothing else; it grants no other meaning to proximity and no proximity to any other semantic | The hard gate. Ten dimensions audited; every one that can be equalised is equalised and asserted. The non-equivalence law was made explicit by `V10-FIX-01`, because the amendment that reserves a relevance semantic must not leave a route by which relevance becomes a score |
| A16 | **Conversation remains primarily conversational.** No fragment, plane, band, role or relationship mark ever appears there | Held with a longer session and a correction in flight |
| A17 | **Live Context is adjacent, lightweight and bounded** — an explicit statement of what is in play now, plus the activation and removal controls the runtime actually supports | Held at the maximum intended handful. See "Rejected" D7 for what is *not* locked about it |
| A18 | **Deep Analysis is the richest composed understanding-space**, and reading roles appear there and nowhere earlier | Asserted live: no role vocabulary in the conversation or the context field |
| A19 | **One intelligence across depths.** A record keeps one identity, one provenance line and one interaction logic on every surface it appears on; depth increases, the object does not change | Followed end-to-end at 375px and desktop with no morph, trail or transition |
| A20 | **A subject anchor organises the analysis when one is available** | Held with a long subject at 375px. The anchor object itself is simulated; the principle is conditional on the runtime one day having it |
| A21 | **Arabic and RTL are first-class, not adapted.** Composed RTL from the start | Held under long subject + long statements + long material simultaneously at 375px |
| A22 | **Contextual Relevance is a reserved future spatial semantic, scoped to Live Context — `RESERVED FUTURE CAPABILITY / SIMULATED ONLY UNTIL RUNTIME SUPPORT EXISTS`.** Inside Live Context, after the reader opens the field, a future runtime-backed system MAY position or recompose items nearer/farther or more/less present **solely** to express how related an item is to what the current conversation is about right now. "Nearer / more present" may mean **only** «more contextually related to the current conversation». It may never mean more important, more true, stronger evidence, higher confidence, higher certainty, higher priority, higher rank or more correct (A15). It is not reader foreground (A9) and never mutates stored state (A10). The scene may recompose as the active conversational context changes, **but only once an explicit, client-consumable runtime contract supplies the relevance signal** — until then it is not production behaviour and must not be claimed as one | Added by `V10-FIX-01`. The independent closure review found the freeze's absolute near/far prohibition over-broad: it blocked a rank, and in doing so also deleted an intended QANDEEL capability that is not a rank. The prohibition that survives is on *ordinal* near/far; relatedness-to-context is reserved, not rejected. Nothing here is exercised by any current build |
| A23 | **A spatial semantic must never be the only carrier of its meaning.** Any future contextual-relevance spatialization (A22) must ship a non-spatial accessible equivalent, must keep that meaning intact and understandable under reduced motion, and must reach keyboard and screen-reader users without requiring them to perceive position, depth, scale or animation | Added by `V10-FIX-01` as a semantic accessibility boundary on the reserved capability, consistent with the accessibility gate this freeze already passed (report §5.9). It is a requirement on the future implementation, not an implementation |

# Open for Final Visual Design

Named questions the next phase inherits, in priority order. Re-prioritised by `V10-FIX-01`.

1. **#1 HIGH-PRIORITY OPEN ITEM — Large / double-digit peer navigation.** Six controls is
   the measured ceiling of the current 375px pager; seven wrap and clip. The runtime caps
   nothing, so it can expose peer counts far above that ceiling. **A3 forbids dropping a
   peer, and no peer may be silently omitted, silently truncated away or made unreachable
   because the peer count grew — every peer must remain reachable at any count.** The
   current six-control pager is prototype evidence of where the present form breaks, not
   the final solution. This is a real product-navigation question, not a styling one, and
   the Final Visual Design phase begins with it. Not solved here, and deliberately so.
2. **The graphic language for the four nominal marks.** What replaces or refines C1–C5 must
   satisfy A6 and A15 (kinds, never amounts), must survive **ten consecutive records
   without becoming texture** — the dense finding — and must reach assistive technology as
   words. This is the largest graphic-language item.
3. **Navigation at density.** Dense analysis is correct and readable and 2,500px long with
   no way to skim. Solve navigation; do not assume a second surface value solves it,
   because comprehension is not what breaks.
4. **If a second surface value is admitted, what it is allowed to mean.** V7's question
   returns intact: is it a *place* or a *level*? V10 did not need one and deliberately did
   not answer it. Whatever answer is chosen must not violate A15.
5. **The numeral policy** — one system, one decision, applied everywhere including record
   dates, times and any future counts (C11).
6. **Live Context's final form.** A17 locks what it is for; what it *looks* like, and
   whether the boundary statement can stop sitting below the fold at five items, is open.
   The reserved contextual-relevance semantic (A22) may be **simulated** here to design the
   final form, under the truth labelling in "Runtime / Truth Boundaries"; designing or
   assuming its runtime contract is not part of this phase's brief.
7. **The app-bar subtitle**, which duplicates the anchor and truncates at 375px. Probably
   deletion.
8. Final palette, typography, iconography, surfaces, Q/logo integration, motion language,
   animation choreography, micro-interaction styling, premium polish, component styling —
   the whole of the next phase's own brief, untouched here.

# Runtime / Truth Boundaries

These bound every locked item above. A frozen principle that violates one of these is not
frozen — it is a bug.

**Backed by the runtime, and therefore expressible:** conversation and its timestamps;
recorded material with a record date, a category and a status, extracted deterministically
from the user's own words; readings held as unranked peers; **any number of readings**, not
two; explicit stored role membership per reading; one record carrying a role in more than
one reading, including the same role in both, and including **some but not all** readings;
assumptions and disconfirming conditions per reading; named unknowns with an
OPEN / RESOLVED / SUPERSEDED lifecycle; explicit user context activation for the four
supported kinds; thin data as a normal state.

**Simulated, and never to be presented as runtime output:** the subject anchor; the Home
surface; product-shaped state fetch; Live Context selection and ordering; recomposition;
exclusion from the current field; foreground and recede; cross-screen continuity; applying
a correction or anything downstream of it; answer-linked gap closure; user-driven reading
status change; cross-session persistence of context activation — **and the stress density
in V10's own fixtures**, which is reviewer test data built to break the grammar and is
declared as such in the page, in the route names and in `TRUTH-MANIFEST.md`.

**Forbidden to infer, in any visual language that succeeds this one:** numeric confidence,
confidence bands or percentages (the quantity does not exist to render); relation strength,
weight, mass or settledness; evidence count read as strength; any ranking; question
utility; **peer order read as rank**; **column or pane length read as strength**; any
relation without a stored role membership; causal arrows or decorative connections between
objects; memory as verbatim quotation; memory-to-turn provenance; record dates read as
event dates; reading states SUPPORTED / MIXED / WEAK / REJECTED / RETIRED; a refusal state
for an unknown; guaranteed distinct readings; **guaranteed two readings**; any structural
consequence of a correction; **and contextual relevance read as any of the above** — a
nearer or more present item never becomes more important, more true, more strongly
supported, more confident, more certain, higher priority, higher rank or more correct
merely by being near.

## Contextual Relevance Spatialization — the runtime gate

Added by `V10-FIX-01`. This bounds A22 and A23.

**CURRENT PRODUCTION — `NOT SUPPORTED`.** The runtime exposes no client-readable relevance
computation suitable for this spatial behaviour. Production's bounded material selection is
a **recency** window, and recency is not a product contract for contextual-relevance
spatialization. Therefore system-driven relevance-based near/far is not supported today, and
production UI must not claim or visually imply that any current near/far position is
runtime-derived contextual relevance. Ordinary composition and responsive layout differences
may of course exist; they must never masquerade as computed relevance.

**DESIGN / PROTOTYPE — `SIMULATED ONLY`.** The next phase may **simulate** the reserved
capability where it is needed to design Live Context's final visual form. Any such fixture
must be declared in reviewer documentation as **SIMULATED FOR EXPERIENCE/DESIGN TESTING**,
in the same way this harness declares its stress fixtures, and must never be presented as
production-backed behaviour.

**FUTURE PRODUCTION — gated.** System-driven contextual-relevance spatialization may become
active only after a real, explicit, client-consumable runtime contract exists with: defined
relevance semantics; a defined source and provenance; deterministic, understood freshness
behaviour; bounded failure and degradation behaviour; no semantic aliasing to confidence,
importance or rank; and an accessible non-spatial equivalent (A23). That contract is not
designed, specified or implied here.

**SCOPE LIMITS — the exception does not generalise.** A22 is one semantic on one surface.

- **One surface.** It reaches Live Context items and nothing else. Peer readings, Deep
  Analysis, the shared region, the conversation and every other surface are outside it, and
  peer equality (A2, A3, A4) is untouched by it. A peer reading may never be positioned,
  ordered or recomposed by relevance.
- **One semantic.** Relevance is the only meaning proximity may carry, and proximity is the
  only channel it may use inside that exception. It does not license a general visual
  weighting system, and it is not, and may not become, a numerical product metric unless a
  future runtime contract explicitly defines one.
- **Direction-independent.** The meaning of nearer and farther is identical in Arabic and
  English, and in RTL and LTR. No reading direction, no edge and no directional convention
  may imply rank or precedence, and the two implementations must carry equivalent product
  meaning rather than mirrored ones.

**Verdict: `FREEZE WITH EXPLICIT OPEN ITEMS`.**

The structural-semantic grammar is ready to be treated as settled. Its current drawing is
not, and deliberately so: freezing the artwork now would constrain the Final Graphic
Language phase with a prototype solution that the density test shows becomes texture, that
the no-explanation test shows does not teach itself, and that the three-peer test shows is
redundant with the words already carrying the meaning.

The next phase inherits a grammar it must express, not a picture it must keep.
