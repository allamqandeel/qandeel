## Copy fidelity

**Five archived files differ substantively from their local source.** They are listed in
full below with the reason and the source SHA-256. The archive does **not** claim that no
substantive content was rewritten: `PHASE V ARCHIVAL FIX-01` deliberately corrected a
factually wrong runtime statement, and truthfulness of the archive takes precedence over
byte identity with the source workspace.

The correction replaced wording that claimed the runtime imposes no cap on readings. The
verified runtime fact is `MAX_ACTIVE_HYPOTHESES = 32`, enforced per user; no per-subject cap
was found. A single subject can therefore plausibly expose peer counts well beyond the
current six-control mobile pager, but the runtime is **not** unlimited, and no document
claims that all 32 belong to one subject. The Phase VI design requirement is unchanged and
unweakened: no peer the product actually exposes may be silently omitted, truncated,
ranked, or made unreachable.
