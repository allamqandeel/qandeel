## 1. Why an amendment is needed

G2.3 froze three Arabic paragraphs in Egyptian, addressing the reader in the masculine («إنك تتعرف عليه»، «تختار
أنت»، «تكون مرتاح ومطمئن»), and withheld any authority for a gender-neutral rewrite. The Product Owner's QANDEEL-wide rule
of 2026-10-13 makes every Matching proposal, system notice and prewritten QANDEEL message fixed copy in neutral or
simplified Modern Standard Arabic, gender-considerate. The Product Owner approved the replacements below.

## 3. What carries over unchanged

- **§1's limits.** The opening is a QANDEEL-initiated moment outside semantic-world geography. It authorizes no
  candidate object, feed or list, no compatibility percentage, score or ranking, no required photo, and no raw private
  reasoning or MY_WORLD evidence.
- **§2's limits.** The Product does not enumerate specific agreements, disagreements, similarities, traits or reasons
  before the Introduction begins (G2 §D). The proposal is not a compatibility report, dashboard, ranking or evidence
  view.
- **§2's copy-only clarification.** «في الوقت المناسب» in the amended M-02 is the same reassurance sentence. It creates no
  runtime capability, architecture requirement, consent mechanism, data-flow requirement or backlog item.
- **The privacy sentence is not weakened.** Private data and analyses are not shown to the other person, and nothing is
  shared except by the reader's own choice («إلا باختيارك»).
- **The process** (§3) and the compact Return presentation (§4 onward) are untouched.
