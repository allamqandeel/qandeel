## The behavioural reference

**F3 — «الفرش ثم التركيز» · Overview ⇄ Focus** is the canonical behavioural reference: the
analysis opens on the whole set of peer readings, and a reading is foregrounded only when the
reader raises it, reversibly, with a permanent named route back to the set.

**F2 — «قراءة مركّزة + مختار الأقران» · Focused Reading + Equal-Peer Chooser** is recorded as
the evaluated runner-up, for provenance only. **It is not the frozen primary architecture.**

## What VI-02 freezes

The behavioural and navigation architecture, at the altitude of *what is true for the reader* —
never at the altitude of what the reader looks at. In summary:

1. When multiple peer readings require choosing, the reader encounters the **whole available
   set** before any reading is system-foregrounded.
2. **No system-selected winner** — no primary, strongest, recommended or relevance-ranked peer.
3. Peers are **equal and unranked**, and are said to be, in words that also reach assistive
   technology.
4. **Every peer the product exposes remains reachable and discoverable.**
5. The **full statement is the current canonical human-facing identity source**; abbreviated
   pointers may never be the sole discriminator on a choosing surface.
6. Where a presentation abbreviates, **full identity is reachable on the choosing surface
   without committing** to that reading.
7. Reader-chosen foreground is **reversible**.
8. **Return to the whole set is continuously available and names its destination.**
9. Restored focus and scroll are **orientation only** — never `current`, primary, active or
   rank.
10. **Deep links may land on a focused reading** only with the whole-set return route intact.
11. **Arabic RTL, English LTR, keyboard and assistive semantics are part of the architecture**,
    not a later remediation pass.
12. **No new runtime label is required** for the validated architecture — scoped by C2 and C3.

The clauses themselves, `F-01 … F-12`, are canonical in
[`VI02_BEHAVIORAL_NAVIGATION_FREEZE.md`](VI02_BEHAVIORAL_NAVIGATION_FREEZE.md).

## What VI-02 explicitly does NOT freeze

**This section is binding.**

Nothing below is settled by VI-02, and none of it may be inferred from the prototype that proved
the behaviour:

- vertical rows;
- paragraph-heavy presentation;
- the white prototype styling;
- cards;
- the number of visible lines;
- final set morphology;
- final small-count composition;
- typography;
- colours;
- surfaces / materials;
- iconography;
- brand expression;
- motion;
- generative graphics;
- final density styling;
- search / filter / jump behaviour;
- visual anchors;
- spatial field;
- circles / grouping;
- relation threads / lines;
- transformation / recomposition animation.

**The row is evidence. It is not the product.** The prototype's peers were drawn as full-width
vertical rows to prove that thirty-two peers stay reachable, equal and identifiable at 375px —
and for no other reason. **Every pixel of it is throwaway.**

### The governing visual-truth rule

> **A visual relationship may be drawn only if it is true of the runtime.**

Grouping asserts belonging. A line asserts a relation. A weight asserts strength. A number
asserts a quantity. Motion asserts that something changed. QANDEEL may make each claim only
where a record supports it. **The gate is truth, not restraint.**

### The product-owner visual guardrail — canonical carry-forward

> The final QANDEEL analysis experience must not default to paragraph/list bureaucracy. VI-03
> and later phases must actively investigate meaningful graphic/spatial structures — circles,
> grouping, threads, anchors, motion, recomposition, and other non-textual structures — when
> they improve attention/comprehension/navigation and remain semantically truthful.

**This guardrail is a permission, not a requirement to add decorative circles or motion.** Each
device must earn its place; none may invent meaning the runtime does not carry.
