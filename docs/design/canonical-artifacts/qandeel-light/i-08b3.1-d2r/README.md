## What changed, in one paragraph

Review found that D2 had attached Product meaning to presentation geometry that nothing
authorised — ring radius as quantity, depth as recency, a contour gap as a sharing fraction,
dimness as how much was known, contour shape as a permanent identity — and that PATTERN inferred
D2 inferred its structure by fitting a principal axis to four screen positions; **both defects are removed.** Every
ambient property now declares what it encodes in a structure the build reads and refuses to
proceed without, and PATTERN draws membership and nothing else. The freeze boundary is stamped on
every token, and the ΔEok 0.020 figure is stated as the engineering heuristic it is rather than a
law. **`D2R_CORRECTION.md` is the record.**

## Four things worth knowing before you look

**Nothing in the ambient field encodes anything, and that is now enforced rather than asserted.**
Every visual property declares `encodes: null` in a structure the build reads; the one exception
names its source, and it says only which world is open. Five guards and a build gate keep it
that way, and they read data structures and written attributes — never their own rule text.

**PATTERN draws a member set.** No axis, no order, no strength, no goodness of fit. Four
identical links to a named locus that is placed where the drawing fits, not where a statistic
puts it.

**The ambient field does not move, and that is the design.** Four independent sources say it
should not — three of them mechanical, including that a breathing ring lands on the exact
frequency two Apple documents name as the one people are most sensitive to, and that such a ring
would not start at all for a user with reduced motion enabled. The richness is in **form**: each
topic is a set of contour lines, drawn the way a hill is drawn on a map. The shape comes from an
**authored presentation seed** belonging to the scene — it is composition, it identifies no topic,
and it promises nothing across releases, devices or users. Measured: **162 resting frames,
byte-identical**, across three worlds.

**The Light colour changed, and it was derived rather than chosen.** I-08B3.1-D1 handed forward
that the diagnostic Light passed within ΔEok 0.0163 of Living Brass — measured here on the full
compositing path, it is **0.0118**, which means LIGHT and MATTER were not separable at exactly
the intensities an arrival decays through. A search found the colour the requirement implies:
hue 89°, five degrees from the family you have already seen, at **0.0201**.
`frames/proof/D2_LIGHT_SEPARATION.png` draws it.

**One contradiction was found inside work that was already accepted.** D1's reduced-motion
counterpart animates a blur, for a good craft reason it states. Apple's Reduce Motion guidance
asks specifically for *"avoiding animating into and out of blurs"*. D2 corrected that one
channel — in the accessibility layer only, which §14 puts inside this stage's remit — and reports
it rather than absorbing it. The full-motion Connection you selected is untouched, and proved so
at fourteen decimal places.

## The typeface is a dependency, not a payload

**No font byte ships here**, and the test is on bytes: any run of base64 that *decodes* to a font
signature would be caught. Estedad v8.5 is recorded with its hash in `data/D2_RESOLUTION.json`
and referenced by `file://` URL.

To open the prototype or the board **with the real face**, Chrome needs one flag:

```
chrome.exe --allow-file-access-from-files "prototypes/D2_LIGHT_SYSTEM.html"
```

Without it the Arabic is still readable, in a fallback face. **The videos and stills carry the
typeface as pixels and are unaffected** — which is why they, and not the prototype, are what the
Product Owner is pointed at.
