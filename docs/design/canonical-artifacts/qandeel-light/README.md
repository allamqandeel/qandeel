# QANDEEL Light — I-08B3.1-D2R Light System

The meaning-light system, with its four semantically separate categories: **AMBIENT**,
**CONNECTION**, **PATTERN** and **INSIGHT**. D2R is the governing state. D2 is not, because its
ambient contract and its whole PATTERN expression were rejected.

- `i-08b3.1-d2r/D2R_FREEZE_CANDIDATE.md`: the four categories, the freeze boundary (13
  product-contract / 32 production-default tokens) and the 15 non-token contract statements.
- `i-08b3.1-d2r/D2R_CORRECTION.md`: what D2R removed from D2 and why. Geometry encodes nothing, and
  PATTERN is membership only.
- `i-08b3.1-d2r/tokens/`: the resolver, the `illumination` base and the dark context.
- `i-08b3.1-d2r/prototypes/D2_LIGHT_SYSTEM.html`: the built prototype.
- `i-08b3.1-d2r/source/`: the scene, the gates, the verifier and the generator (with the D1 and C3
  inputs they vendor).

Provenance, the D2 / D2R resolution and exclusions: [`SOURCE-PROVENANCE.md`](SOURCE-PROVENANCE.md).
