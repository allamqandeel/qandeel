## B. What R3 records (brief §19)

### B1. The exact new-conversation opener

```text
اهلا يا {display_name} ... انا في انتظارك ... يلا نبدأ
Hi {display_name} ... I'm here waiting for you ... let's get started
```

**How it is shown:**
- It is QANDEEL's first turn in **every new normal conversation**, on QANDEEL's side, with the Q once above it.
- It is **not** the post-registration Welcome, onboarding, or Product-definition copy.
- `{display_name}` is the only substitution. The name is `<bdi>`-isolated; that changes rendering, not text.

**How it is checked:**
- R3-01a compares the UTF-8 bytes against the brief's wording, which is typed independently into the checker.
- R3-01b reads the rendered paragraph back in Arabic, in English, and in Arabic with a Latin display name.
- The probes are «انا مستنيك» and a hamza-«corrected» version; both are rejected.

### B2. The selected partial slab

**Geometry (logical, so it mirrors by itself):**
- It is set on the one functional Surface tone.
- It bleeds through the 24 px gutter to the reader's own screen edge (`margin-inline-start: -24px`).
- It is square and open on that edge, and rounded 18 px only on the two corners facing into the conversation.
- Padding is 11 / 12 block and 24 (edge) / 18 (inward) inline.
- `max-width: calc(100% - 40px)`, which always leaves QANDEEL a lane of **≥ 64 px**. The measured minimum is 88 px, over 127 turns on 16 states (R3-02).

**Rhythm and ink:**
- Consecutive reader turns sit 6 px apart; an exchange is 28 px.
- Both speakers use primary ink at weight 400.
- Under Increase Contrast, a 1 px boundary is drawn on the three inward sides only.

**Checks:**
- **R3-05:** inward corners ≥ 12 px, edge corners 0, bleed −24 px, on every reader turn in 16 states. Probe: a full-radius bubble is rejected.
- **A11:** the only fill is `var(--functional)`; no shadow, literal colour, Brass or status colour.

### B3. The Arabic / English speaker-side rule

**Rule:**

| UI | USER | QANDEEL |
|---|---|---|
| Arabic | the **RIGHT** edge (the START edge in RTL) | the opposite side |
| English | the **LEFT** edge (the START edge in LTR) | the opposite side |

**How QANDEEL sits:**
- QANDEEL is open on the World, never contained.
- A 48 px start inset keeps every QANDEEL line **≥ 72 px** clear of the reader's edge.
- A short reply shrink-wraps, so it visibly sits opposite.

**Bidi:** paragraph direction is independent of side.
- Each turn container takes the phone's direction.
- Only the paragraph takes its own direction, decided by the script that carries the sentence (`src/bidi.js`).

**Checks:**
- **R3-02** covers 16 states and every turn. Probes: A1's placement, and a container `dir` flip.
- **R3-03** covers the mixed set. Probe: first-strong `dir="auto"`.

### B4. The Writing / Voice Note / Live Call model

These are three ways of talking to one QANDEEL in one conversation history, not three products. All three start from **one lower line**: the composer, a B4 FIELD drawn as a single line.

| Mode | Where it starts | Visible surface | History |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Writing** | The line itself; Send appears when there is text | **Conversation-first.** Upper action «تحليل المحادثة» | Durable turns |
| **Voice Note** | The microphone at the line's end. The line becomes a recording line: «بسجّل», elapsed time, level; Cancel / Send | **Conversation-first.** Recording never moves the reader (R3-07 records the depth while recording) | A voice turn on the reader's side: play, static waveform, duration, committed text. QANDEEL's spoken reply opposite, same form |
| **Live Call** | The handset beside the microphone | **Analysis-first** (§B5) | A live marker «مكالمة صوتية بدأت» while running; afterwards a record line «مكالمة صوتية · m:ss» |

The controls are two icon buttons, 44 × 44 and 8 px apart, at the end of the line. There is no toolbar and no mode switcher, and the rail never gains a mode (A02, R3-07).

### B5. Live Call = Analysis-first, and one call across both depths

**What happens:**
- Starting a call commits `call = live` and `depth = Analysis`.
- The Analysis rises, using G1.1's lift / rise with no new motion.
- The line becomes the call line: «مكالمة صوتية», call time, level, mute, and end call.

**In the Analysis:**
- The upper chrome shows «المحادثة», with the accessible name «المحادثة — المكالمة مستمرة», plus Replay.
- «المحادثة» opens the Conversation **without ending the call**: the call bar never moves, and «تحليل المحادثة» returns to the **same** call.

**The rules that keep it one call:**
- `enterWorld` / `leaveWorld` never touch the call state.
- Escape leaves the Analysis but never ends a call.
- Ending the call leaves the reader where they are; they are not moved.

**Checked by R3-08:**
- Analysis on screen with end call, mute and the route reachable.
- Conversation with the call still live.
- A return with an unchanged call start instant.
- Probes: "leaving ends the call" and "returning restarts it"; both are rejected.

### B6. Conversation history continuity

- There is one thread: yesterday's conversation, today's opener, writing, voice notes, the call marker and record, and whatever the reader sends live all land in the same `.thread`, on the correct side.
- A reader who is at the latest line stays pinned there when the call marker arrives. A reader who has scrolled back is not moved.
- Returning from the Analysis lands on the exact scroll position it was left at, as in G1.1.

### B7. Replay placement

**Where it sits:**
- One quiet icon control (a media glyph, never mirrored) at the **END edge of the upper chrome**.
- It is in the identical box in the Conversation, the Analysis and during a call (R3-09). Probe: moving it in the Analysis is rejected.
- It is not a tab, a World or a mode; the rail stays the three Worlds.

**Availability:**
- It is **not advertised** on a brand-new conversation: the opener is not the reader's committed material. It is hidden visually and from the keyboard.
- It becomes reachable with the reader's first committed words (R3-10b). Its slot is reserved, so nothing shifts when it appears.

**The flow:**
1. **Entry, an ASIDE:** «المحادثة كاملة» / «جزء من المحادثة».
2. **«جزء من المحادثة»:** a selection mode on the conversation's own turns. Each turn is a checkbox; tap the first and last. The range is contiguous and in source order, with a true count («4 رسائل»).
3. **«معاينة»:** the preview PASSAGE, which states its boundary.

There is **no** timeline, trim or editor (R3-13a).

**How it maps to the runtime:**
- «المحادثة كاملة» ↔ `FULL_SOURCE`.
- «جزء من المحادثة» ↔ `SELECTED_EXCERPT` over `EXPLICIT_RESOLVED_ANCHORS` (replay-runtime-v1 §5).

### B8. Replay runtime truth and the audio dependency

What `docs/replay-runtime-v1.md` establishes, and this proof respects:
- **A Replay is a material artifact, never a World (§1).**
- **Supported source (§7):** Personal committed text (`MY_WORLD`) is SUPPORTED.
- **Not producible:**
  - Personal original audio / call is **NOT PRODUCIBLE**: "no durable canonical source exists" (§7).
  - Nothing may invent audio or promote a transcript to audio.
  - The open Live Head cannot be frozen (§19).
- **Distribution fails closed in production (§62).**

The proof therefore designs the Replay control and flow but claims no audio export. R3-10a checks that no Replay string implies audio, and that the preview states the boundary. The dependency, verbatim from the brief:

> The intended Product Replay — original conversation/call audio with the Living Analysis world as the visual hero — requires the Voice / Live Call track to establish a reviewed durable Personal original-audio/call source before launch.

The same dependency applies to **playing back a Personal voice note from the history**. `conversation_units.source_modality` is CHECK-pinned to TEXT (migration 0064). This proof shows the voice turn's media row as the intended representation; it does not claim it is backed today.

### B9. Count-correct shared-area grammar

- **The rail is count-neutral:** «مع الآخرين» / "With others", at every count. R3-11a verifies the label contains no singular or plural World noun that a count could falsify.
- **The heading is derived** from the rows actually shown:

| Rows shown | Heading |
|---|---|
| 0 | the rail label, plus «لسه مفيش عالم مشترك.» |
| 1 | «عالم مشترك» |

- R3-11b renders 0 / 1 / 3 in Arabic and 1 / 3 in English. Probe: a plural heading over one row is rejected.

### B10. B4 reconciliation status

The slab truthfully fits **none** of the four B4 roles. APPARATUS and FIELD both forbid committed conversational content; ASIDE and PASSAGE are transient.

- **Recommendation:** one additive, same-tone role, **UTTERANCE**. It changes no existing screen and no token value.
- **Status:** **not frozen.** The slab is an approved visual candidate, and the prototype resolves it from `qandeel.surface.functional` directly rather than borrowing a role.
- **Detail:** `G1.1_R3_B4_RECONCILIATION_NOTE.md`. This supersedes A1's J5 entry.

## F. Accessibility (R3 §18)

**Speakers:**
- **Not only colour:** speaker identity uses position plus ground, with no hue (R3-04).
- **Not only visual:** every turn, including voice notes and the opener, announces «كلامك» / «قنديل» (English: You / Qandeel) before its words (R3-12a).

**Controls:**
- **Names:** every control has an accessible name and passes Label-in-Name (B06, B07). The play controls carry the duration.
- **Targets:** every visible control is ≥ 44 × 44 at 100% and 200% text (B08).
- **Reach:** each mode's controls and Replay are reachable and not inert (R3-12b).

**Text and contrast:**
- Arabic leading is ≥ 1.63 everywhere (B04). There is no overflow at 200% text or at 360 px (B05).
- Every contrast pair meets its threshold in both appearances (A08), including the voice-note transcript and duration on the slab, and the part-selection marks.

**Keyboard and motion:**
- Escape unwinds the innermost layer only.
- Reduced Motion removes no capability (R3-M1).
- **Not verified:** VoiceOver / TalkBack on real devices, React Native per-Text direction on Android, OLED behaviour of the one-tone step. These remain a device gate.

## G. Motion (R3 §17)

**What moves:**
- R3 adds **no duration and no curve**: one curve (T-10) and G1.1's durations (A06).
- Three things move, each named **spatial consistency**: the Live Call depth change, the line changing mode, and the Replay ASIDE.
- Nothing in the Conversation is decorative, and there is no loop on a live call.

**Order and Reduced Motion:**
- State is committed before any frame moves. The truth record at "commit" already carries the new depth and call.
- Reduced Motion replaces travel with a 140 ms resolve.
- **R3-M1** compares the prototype's own truth records, 16 in total (8 acts × commit and settled), between `journey-ar-dark` and `journey-ar-dark-reduced-motion`. They agree on depth, call state, composer mode, Replay availability and history everywhere, and on the visible surface and reachable controls once settled.

## H. React Native mapping notes (for the implementing task; not built here)

**State and navigation:**
- The depth, the call and the Replay entry are **ProductRoot state**, not routes: G1.1 found that `_layout.tsx` / `index.tsx` forbid a product route tree.
- If a route tree is later allowed, the Replay **preview** maps to a native-stack `presentation: 'formSheet'` (react-navigation form-sheet). The **entry** stays an anchored popover-style ASIDE, not a sheet.

**Audio:**
- Voice Note and Live Call share **one** recorder and **one** audio context, suspended when idle. The waveform of a sent note is static, so no per-frame allocation is needed (react-native-best-practices › audio).

**Direction:**
- React Native has no per-Text `direction` on Android. The proposed route is a render-time RLM / LRM prefix per paragraph from `paragraphDir`. This is a device gate.

**Motion:**
- The depth lift and rise are transform and opacity only, suitable for Reanimated on the UI thread.
- Reduced Motion uses the system flag, and the call's level line flattens under it.
