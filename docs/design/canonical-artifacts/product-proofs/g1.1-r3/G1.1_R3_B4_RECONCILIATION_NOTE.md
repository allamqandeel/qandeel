# G1.1-R3 — B4 Surface-contract reconciliation note (the reader's conversational slab)

**Status: RECOMMENDATION FOR INDEPENDENT REVIEW. Nothing here is frozen, and B4 is not changed.** Until
review decides, the slab is an approved **visual candidate** (R3 §14), not authority to change B4.

This note answers the four questions in R3 §14. It supersedes the short J5 entry in the A1 package.

## 1. Can the slab be an existing role, used as it is, without changing what that role means?

**No.** Each of the four roles either forbids what the slab carries or requires behaviour the slab does not have.

| Role | Its defining cause (B0R) | Why the slab is not it |
|---|---|---|
| **APPARATUS** | "standing machinery … not content of the world" | **Must NOT contain:** "content of any kind, analytical or conversational". The slab holds conversational content. |
| **ASIDE** | "anchored … transient … light-dismiss" | The slab is permanent history. Nothing summons it and nothing dismisses it. |
| **PASSAGE** | "the world is suspended" (modal, with a scrim) | The slab blocks nothing. |
| **FIELD** | "your words stop here — the authorship / commit boundary" | **Must NOT contain:** "committed content". The slab holds exactly the reader's committed words. |

FIELD comes closest in spirit, because the slab is the reader's words standing on the material they were written on. But B0R §3.4 explicitly excludes committed content from FIELD. Calling the slab FIELD would silently redefine FIELD to cover history. It would also weaken FIELD's own guarantee: a Surface that marks exactly what *will be* sent.

The Earning Test gives the same answer:
- **Clause 1, REMOVAL:** remove the slab and speaker attribution at a glance fails. The A1 before/after and five-system boards show this.
- **Clause 2, CHEAPER MECHANISM:** tested, not assumed. Position alone was built and measured in A1 and is weaker at a glance.
- **Clause 4, NON-SEMANTIC:** the slab says nothing about analysis.
- **Clause 5, BOUNDARY DUTY:** the duty is *who said this*.
- **Clause 3, ROLE:** "exactly one of APPARATUS, ASIDE, PASSAGE or FIELD must apply". **This is the only clause the slab fails.**

The prototype therefore does **not** borrow a role for the slab. `src/tokens.mjs` resolves the slab fill directly from `qandeel.surface.functional`, not through `qandeel.role.apparatus.fill` as A1 did. Using the APPARATUS alias would have claimed the slab is machinery.

## 2. If not, is one narrowly scoped new conversational role actually required?

**Yes.** The duty is real, and no existing cause covers it: *attribution of committed human words inside a conversation*. The only alternative is to leave clause 3 failing, and a contract that its own design fails is no longer a contract. Keeping both the slab and the ROLE clause as it stands requires one new role.

The reverse option, dropping the slab and going back to position only, is the documented fallback. R3 §3 rules it out except "as a documented fallback by a real canonical conflict". Here the conflict can be removed by a small, additive change, so the fallback is not needed.

## 3. What is the minimum contract change?

**Recommendation: one additive role, `UTTERANCE`.** It adds nothing to the tone system and redefines no existing role.

```text
UTTERANCE — "these words are the reader's"
  cause        attribution of the reader's own committed words in a conversation history
  may contain  exactly one committed human turn (text, or a voice message with its committed text)
  must NOT     analysis of any kind; QANDEEL's words; controls other than the turn's own media control;
               in-progress (uncommitted) text — that is FIELD
  nature       persistent, non-interactive as a container, anchored to the author's own screen edge
  relationship inset in the World's content plane; never over it
  scrim        never
  may sit on   WORLD only
  tone         {qandeel.surface.functional} — the same one tone (no new value; B4 one-tone rule intact)
  geometry     bleeds to the author's edge; rounded only on the inward corners (R3 §3)
```

The concrete edits:
1. **B0R ontology:** a fifth §3 entry with the text above.
2. **B4 canonical spec:**
   - §2 (the one-tone diagram) gains a fifth alias, `qandeel.role.utterance.fill`, pointing into the same `qandeel.surface.functional`.
   - §3 gains a fifth row. **Told apart by:** "anchored to its author's own screen edge, inside the content plane, under committed human words". **Never told apart by:** "a second tone, a shadow, a colour or Brass, a four-corner capsule".
   - The row states the inward-only 18 px radius. §3 lists "a card radius" among what APPARATUS is never told apart by; the slab is not a card, because its edge side stays square and open.
3. **Tokens:** one alias, `qandeel.role.utterance.fill = {qandeel.surface.functional}`. **No new colour** — A01 and A11 verify the value is the existing functional tone.
4. **Earning Test clause 3:** "exactly one of APPARATUS, ASIDE, PASSAGE, FIELD or UTTERANCE".
5. **Proof code:** the alias becomes the slab's route in place of the direct base token. This is a one-line change in `src/tokens.mjs`.

**Rejected alternative: treat the slab as a content-plane tone, not a Surface.** This avoids a fifth role, but it breaks B4's rule that any tone step in the World is a Surface. That rule is what makes the one-tone system mechanically inspectable. It is a larger change disguised as a smaller one.

## 4. Does the change affect any other screen?

**Existing screens: no.** The change is purely additive. No existing Surface is reclassified, no role loses a case, and no token value changes. The composer stays FIELD, the rail APPARATUS, the Replay entry ASIDE and the preview PASSAGE.

**Screens that will inherit it:**

- **Voice notes in the Personal history.** Already covered: a voice message is a turn (R3 §7). See `proof/modes/R3-06-voice-note.png`.
- **Shared World conversation.** Several humans speak there, so "the reader's own edge" needs a rule for other people's turns. UTTERANCE as written covers the reader only. Other humans' turns are **not** decided here, because deciding them would invent Shared-World Product logic. That remains open for the Shared World conversation surface.
- **Replay preview (G2).** If a Replay renders the conversation, the preview should inherit the same attribution role.
- **The shipped T-08 conversation screens.** They will need the slab when the shell is implemented. That is an implementation task, not a contract change.
