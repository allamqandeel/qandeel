## Decision A

**Analysis shell stays dark with the Living Analysis World under both system appearances, while non-Analysis surfaces
continue following their normal system appearance.**

### What the proof now does

- **While the Analysis is shown, under system Light**, these take the dark scope together with the world:
  - the status region;
  - the upper chrome;
  - the Replay action and its entry;
  - the Timeline and OrientationChrome, which were already dark;
  - the call line;
  - the rail;
  - the home indicator;
  - the phone's ground.

  The whole screen reads as one immersive place. Status text and icons use the dark scope's light ink.
- **Under system Dark** nothing changes from G3.1.
- **What keeps following the system:** the Conversation, the private proposal and the Shared World, under Light as under
  Dark. A Live Call keeps one identity across Analysis (dark) → Conversation (light, call line included) → Analysis
  (dark).
- **The tone change at the surface boundary** is F2's own appearance cross-fade quantity:
  - `qandeel.appearance.switch.crossfade` = 200 ms, the same in both directions;
  - it is removed (a cut) under Reduced Motion, as F2 requires.

### What it is not

- **Not an app-wide dark mode.** Nothing outside the Analysis changes.
- **Not a user appearance setting.** No control exists, in the Product or in the harness.
- **Not a change to any F2 token.** The dark scope is F2's own dark palette, applied to the shell around the world.
- **Not a Light repaint of I-08B1, and not a veil.** The world alone is 0 px different between Dark and Light, still
  and in motion (K07).
- **Not in the harness.** G3.1's `?shell=A|B` fixture is gone. Its failing answer (B) survives only as planted
  probes PR1 and PR5, which the checks reject.

## Decision B

**Temporal orientation context is visually reconnected to the Timeline by placing the concise temporal
orientation/status line directly above the Timeline when that context genuinely exists.**

### The composition

The narrowest truthful composition was attempted first, and it held:

```
Living Analysis World
→ temporal orientation line            (only while PINNED, or while previewing a Moment)
→ Timeline / Temporal Surface           (the Live-edge act at the END edge, the Track)
→ OrientationChrome                     (the inspection line; «طرق العودة» and every other Return act)
```

- **The line.** Its words are T-08's, unchanged:
  - «أنت عند اللحظة 14. استمرت المحادثة بعد هذه اللحظة.», with the second sentence only when the conversation did
    continue;
  - or the preview line «نظرة مؤقتة على اللحظة 15، ولم يتغير موضعك.»;
  - in English, T-08's English.

  They are said **once**. OrientationChrome no longer says them.
- **Its place.** It is the Timeline's first child. It stands on the Timeline row's free top 28 pt and grows upward.
  It sits directly above the Live-edge act's label (≈ 9 pt of air between ink), and rides with the Timeline.
- **Its presentation.**
  - **Type and inset:** the chrome's text style (`r-support`), with the same 24-pt start inset as the line it replaces.
    Its end runs to 12 pt from the edge, a ragged end that keeps it to two lines at 320 pt (F-09).
  - **Ground:** while it is shown, the world's own legibility falloff gives it the same ground every chrome word has.
    The world is not touched.
  - **Motion:** a 220 ms resolve in and a 140 ms resolve out. Under Reduced Motion: a 140 ms resolve in and a cut
    out. It never translates on its own.
- **What does not move.** «طرق العودة» and the other Return acts stay in OrientationChrome, under the T-11 amendment.
  - T-08's offered set, order, labels, hints and executors are untouched, and equal to G3.1's at every paired state
    (K12).
  - Return Live stays in its one home, the Live edge.
- **One presentation adjustment the move required.** The Live-edge act's hit box now begins at its label row (60 of
  the row's 88 pt). Its focus ring therefore never crosses the line (finding F-02). The label and mark do not move.
- **Paid by the support, never by the world.** The line's height above the Timeline row is charged to the support
  before OrientationChrome gets its room, so the world keeps `max(160, usable / 2)` in every capture (K14).
  - At 320 × 568 in a live call while PINNED, the world sits exactly on its 160-pt floor.
  - OrientationChrome keeps one act row there and scrolls inside it.
  - The line and Return Live never scroll.

### The PINNED reading, in one local cluster

| Question | Where it is answered |
|---|---|
| Where in time | «أنت عند اللحظة 14.» |
| The conversation continued after that point | «استمرت المحادثة بعد هذه اللحظة.» |
| Where the Live edge is | directly beneath, at the END edge, with its mark |
| How to return to Live | «العودة إلى المحادثة الجارية», the Live-edge act itself |

The Timeline remains subordinate to the world. The line is a sentence on the world's material: no card, no header, no
banner, no badge.
