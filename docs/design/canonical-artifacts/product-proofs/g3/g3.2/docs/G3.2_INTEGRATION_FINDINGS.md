## Findings

### F-01 · `INTEGRATION CRAFT DEFECT` · corrected — the moved line had less ground than every other chrome word

- **What was found, on sight.** G3.1's world legibility falloff reaches `.62` at the top of the Timeline row.
  - Once the temporal line stood there, a world label («جلسة ١٢») showed through the sentence at 320 × 568 in a
    call while PINNED.
  - The world had not changed: the line had simply moved onto thinner material.
- **Correction.** While the line is shown (`--ctx-on`, its own opacity), the falloff's top stop rises from `.62` to
  `.88` and sits at the line's top edge. With no temporal context the falloff is exactly G3.1's.
  - This is the world material thinning over the cosmos, which G2.1 already uses. It is not a Surface, a card or a
    veil. The world itself is not touched: world-only renders are 0 px different (K06).
- **Evidence:** K26, which measures the line's ink against the ground actually painted behind it at every size and
  language, and must reach at least 4.5 : 1. Boards 02 and 06.

### F-02 · `INTEGRATION CRAFT DEFECT` · corrected — the Live-edge act's box would have enclosed the moved line

- **What was found.** G3.1's PINNED Live-edge act was 88 pt tall: the whole Timeline row, with its label at the row's
  middle.
  - The moved line now sits in that row's free top 28 pt, so the act's hit box would have covered the sentence.
  - Its keyboard focus ring would have crossed the sentence too.
- **Correction.** The act's box now begins at its label row: 60 pt, still ≥ 44 × 44. The label and its mark do not
  move, and nothing about the act changes.
- **Evidence:** live step B-PINNED "the Live-edge act's box and focus ring do not overlap the line"; board 11 (focus
  on the Live-edge act).

### F-03 · `INTEGRATION CRAFT DEFECT` · corrected — at the tightest room, OrientationChrome's soft edge faded a fully visible act

- **What was found.** G3.1 marked OrientationChrome as "more below" whenever its scroll height exceeded its room.
  - At 320 × 568 in a call while PINNED, only the room's bottom padding overflows: «طرق العودة» is fully
    visible.

- **Correction.** The edge now appears only when a line or an act is actually cut.
- **Evidence:** K15; board 06; clip 05.

### F-04 · `INTEGRATION CRAFT DEFECT` · corrected — the shell's tone change was not F2's appearance cross-fade

- **What was found.** In G3.1 the shell blended to dark over 200 ms on entering the Analysis, but back to light over
  320 ms after a 100 ms delay. That was the depth travel's timing, not an appearance quantity.
- **Why it matters now.** Once Decision A makes the shell's tone a Product rule, its change at the
  Analysis ↔ Conversation boundary is an appearance change. F2 owns that: `qandeel.appearance.switch.crossfade` =
  200 ms, symmetric, and removed under Reduced Motion.
- **Correction.** 200 ms in both directions, and a cut under Reduced Motion.
- **Evidence:** K21 (measured from clip frames); boards 08 and 12.

### F-09 · `INTEGRATION CRAFT DEFECT` · corrected — in English, the line pushed the world below its floor at 320 × 568

- **What was found.** Check K14, on the first full capture run, found it; no eye would have.
  - At 320 × 568, in a live call, while PINNED, T-08's English temporal sentence wrapped to **three** lines on the
    272-pt measure.
  - G3.1's fixed OrientationChrome minimum (54 pt, then 46 in the first G3.2 draft) no longer fitted in what the
    support had left.
  - The world fell to 139 pt against its 160-pt floor.
- **Correction, two parts.**
  - **The line's measure.** It keeps its 24-pt start inset but runs to 12 pt from the end edge (a ragged end, no end
    padding). That gives a 284-pt measure, which holds the sentence to two lines in both languages.
  - **The yield order, from T-11 §3's own text.** The fixed minimum is gone. OrientationChrome is the region that
    yields: it clips to the room it is given and keeps its remainder reachable inside itself. The world's floor
    wins.
- **What remains true after the fix.**
  - In every captured state OrientationChrome still has at least one full act row.
  - A state with less room than that would scroll «طرق العودة» inside a smaller room rather than squeeze the world. No
    state in this proof reaches that (F-08).
- **Evidence:**
  - K14: 0 failing captures; the tightest are the call-PINNED states at 320.
  - The planted probe **PR10** restores the fixed minimum and is rejected by K14.
