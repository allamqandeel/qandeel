## A. What G1.2 proves, in one screen of text

QANDEEL has three ways of talking inside **one** Conversation.

- **Writing** is the line itself. **Voice Note** (microphone) and **Live Call** (handset) are two icon controls at the
  line's end: different names, shapes, places and outcomes.
- **Writing and Voice Note are Conversation-first.** A committed voice note is the reader's `UTTERANCE`: same side, same
  one-tone slab. It carries its play control, its measured duration and «رسالة صوتية». There is **no transcript** (none
  exists) and **no waveform** (it would be invented data).
- **A Live Call STARTS Analysis-first.** The Analysis is the hero, and the call is the one line: its state in words, its
  time, the microphone level, and speaker / mute / end.
  - «المحادثة» opens the Conversation without ending the call. «تحليل المحادثة» returns to the same call.
  - The Analysis picture never moves with sound. «سياق الكلام» changes only when a spoken turn **commits**.
- **The call continues** when the screen locks, auto-locks or another app comes to the front.
- **RETURN restores the last truthful in-call surface.** A lock on the Analysis returns to the Analysis; another app
  over the Conversation returns to the Conversation. Same call id, same Conversation id, one call session.
- **If the call no longer exists,** QANDEEL says so: the Conversation, V07 «المكالمة وقفت. الكتابة متاحة.», Writing,
  and **no live control**. A force-quit relaunch shows no call at all.
- **The microphone is asked for at the moment of intent, never at launch.** Denial leaves Writing with the alternative
  named.
- **Production path (store-safe):** iOS `playAndRecord` / `voiceChat`, the `audio` background mode and a CallKit outgoing
  call. Android `RECORD_AUDIO` at intent, Core-Telecom, a foreground service started while visible, and a CallStyle
  ongoing-call notification (transparency, not a defect). Every background capability maps to the real user-visible
  Live Call and nothing else. `expo-audio` alone is not the call stack.

**Verification:** every quality gate Q1–Q12 has at least one check, and every defeatable check has a planted-defect
probe (§N; `data/G12_CHECKS.json`).

## C. Control architecture (brief §13) — "I can type, send a recorded voice message, or start talking live"

- **One line, three ways.** The idle line reads «كلامك هنا». At its end, 44 px apart by 8 px, sit the handset
  «مكالمة صوتية» and the microphone «رسالة صوتية». Typing turns the microphone into Send.
- **Why this and not a toolbar:**
  - the three ways are not three products;
  - two familiar glyphs carry the difference;
  - the outcomes differ where the reader looks: the microphone keeps the Conversation, the handset opens the Analysis.
- **Rejected:** three equal-weight buttons (telecom dashboard), a mode switcher, a long-press-only microphone, and Live
  Call hidden in overflow.
- **Evidence:** `writ-02`, `writ-01`; board `writing/G12-01`; check **G12-Q2**, with a probe planting "same name,
  same glyph" that is rejected.

## D. Writing (brief §14)

Writing is unchanged from G1.1: the calm baseline. The voice controls add nothing to the upper chrome and nothing above
the line until they are used. The notice and the state strip appear only when there is something to say. Replay keeps
its place (check **G12-R1**).

- **No precision gesture.** Tap to start, tap to send or cancel; long-press is never the only method.
- **Removed from G1.1-R3.** The seeded waveform (fake data) and the fixture transcript line (no STT exists).

**Recommendation R-VN — a Voice Note when QANDEEL leaves the screen (brief §15, explicit review item).** Leaving
QANDEEL mid-capture — lock, another app, a call — **stops the capture and keeps it as an unsent draft**:

- The line says «توقّف التسجيل عند الخروج من قنديل» in full (the strip), keeps the duration, and offers send / discard.
- **Why:**
  1. The locked background promise is the Live Call's alone.
  2. A voice note is a message the reader composes while looking; nothing a reader has not sent may be sent, and
     nothing may keep recording unseen.
  3. On Android a background microphone would need its own foreground service and Play declaration for a feature that
     does not need it. On iOS it would stretch the `audio` mode past the call (compliance note §1.4, §2.2).
  4. Discarding would silently lose the reader's words.
- **Alternatives for review:** discard on leave; or continue in the background with its own OS indication. Both are
  rejected here. Decision **G12-J1**.
- **Evidence:** `vn-07`; **G12-VN**, where a planted "send on leave" is rejected; `voiceNoteBackgroundCapture: false` in
  the compliance table.

## F. Live Call (brief §16–17, §20, §23)

- **Entry.** The handset is a deliberate act. If the microphone is undecided, the OS prompt comes first and **nothing
  starts until it is answered**. Denied → the Conversation with Writing (`pf-02`, `pf-01`; journey D1).
- **START = Analysis-first** (`lc-01` connecting → `lc-02` live; **G12-Q3**). The depth change is G1.1's lift / rise.
- **Controls, minimal and supporting the hero:**
  - end call (the laid-down handset in a ring: a shape, not red);
  - mute (slashed microphone, `aria-pressed`);
  - speaker toggle (filled / outline cone, `aria-pressed`; **CANDIDATE**, default ON because an Analysis-first call is
    watched, not held to the ear — **G12-J4**);
  - «المحادثة» in the upper chrome;
  - Replay in its G1.1 place.
- **Words for every phase** (all **OPEN** VI-01 §14 wording):

  | Phase | Arabic | English |
  |---|---|---|
  | connecting | «جاري الاتصال» | Connecting |
  | live, microphone on | «الميكروفون شغّال» (mechanical, not «بسمعك») | Microphone on |
  | QANDEEL speaking | «قنديل بيتكلم» (V05) | Qandeel is speaking |
  | muted | «الميكروفون مكتوم» | Microphone muted |
  | reconnecting | V06 «الاتصال انقطع. جاري إعادة الاتصال.» | The call dropped. Reconnecting. |
  | failed | V07 «المكالمة وقفت. الكتابة متاحة.» | The call isn't working. You can keep going in writing. |

  «بسجّل» is never used for a call (**G12-08**). "Thinking" is never used (**G12-07**).
- **The line is the microphone.** It draws the reader's level while the microphone carries speech, and lies flat while
  QANDEEL speaks. QANDEEL's own audio is **never** drawn as a meter: no orb, no equalizer, no glow (**G12-05**).
- **Reconnection / failure.** A drop is named and the recovered call is the **same** call. A failed reconnection returns
  to the Conversation with V07, Writing, the record «… · وقفت», every committed turn kept, and **no call control**
  (`pf-03`, `pf-04`; journey D2; **G12-Q10**).
- **Ended by the reader** (`lc-10`): the reader stays where they are (G1.1), and the history keeps «مكالمة صوتية · m:ss».
  **Rule proposed (G12-J3):** an end the reader did *not* choose returns them to the Conversation, where the named
  alternative is actually on screen.
- **Interruption / barge-in (§21).** The Foundation Freeze fixes the principle (interruption is first-class; QANDEEL
  yields immediately). **Detection and semantics are an OPEN RUNTIME DEPENDENCY.** One clearly marked **CANDIDATE**
  (`lc-09`): QANDEEL's audio stops and the microphone carries the reader. Nothing is frozen (G12-J5).
- **Held by another call.** A system call, Siri or an alarm interrupting QANDEEL is a real Product state that G1.2 does
  **not** design (compliance note §1.6, §2.6). It is recorded as **G12-J6**, not mapped onto "reconnecting".

## G. Background and restore (brief §4–5, §18–19) — the locked requirement

- **The rule, implemented in the prototype runtime:** backgrounding and returning **never write the surface**.
  `toBackground` records the away time; `toForeground` re-commits and changes nothing, while the call is genuinely
  active.
- **Journey C** (`proof/motion/C-background-restore-ar.mp4`, standard and Reduced Motion, plus English):
  1. call → Analysis-first;
  2. **lock** → the call continues (the OS schematic);
  3. **return** → the same Analysis, same call id, 3 s of call time later (and «سياق الكلام» moved on with turns
     committed while away);
  4. «المحادثة» → **another app** → **return** → the **Conversation**, not the Analysis;
  5. «تحليل المحادثة» → the same call.
  - Check **G12-Q4Q5** proves it on the live prototype, with probes planting "reset to the Analysis on return", "the call
    ends on background" and "return starts a new call session", all rejected. **G12-M2** reads it from the truth logs.
- **Truthful non-continuity:**
  - A call that **ended while QANDEEL was away** returns to the Conversation with V07 and no live control (`bg-05`,
    journey D3).
  - A **force-quit** relaunch shows no call, no control and no invented record (`bg-06`). No survival after force-quit
    is promised (brief §4).
- **OS-owned vs Product-owned (§19).** The lock screen, the system call UI, the microphone indicator, Android's
  ongoing-call notification, Task Manager and the route picker are drawn **only** as grey, dashed, labelled schematics.
  They are not QANDEEL material and not an imitation of any OS. The in-app before and after states are the Product
  proof. Return paths (app icon, recents, the call notification / system call UI) all land on the same rule.
- **Production mapping:** compliance note §6 (the legal timeline) and dependency note §3 (one in-process call
  controller; no background work except the call's audio).

## H. The Analysis during a call (brief §24, §26)

- **Not redesigned.** The G1.1 scaffold is kept: the sealed F2 fixture world. G2 owns the richness.
- **Bounded truthful proof data.** «سياق الكلام» (VI-01 approved vocabulary for Live Context) names the reading the
  latest **committed** spoken turn brought into play. Every value is a reading actually drawn in the fixture world
  («الرغبة في الإتقان», «قلّة النوم», «إرهاق آخر الأسبوع»); no relation is invented.
- **Check G12-AN** samples 40 s of live call every 100 ms:
  - the picture never changes;
  - «سياق الكلام» changes only within 100 ms after each commit, and never at a speech edge;
  - nothing changes while the line is down or at reconnection.
  - A planted "Analysis reacts to QANDEEL's voice" is rejected.

## I. Replay coexistence (brief §28)

- **Placement:** Replay stands at the identical position in the Conversation, the Analysis, and both during a call. The
  call controls never collide with it (**G12-R1**; a planted "moves during a call" is rejected).
- **During a call:** its entry says «المكالمة الجارية غير مشمولة.»
- **What it binds:** call markers are not selectable turns. Replay binds committed material only; Personal call audio is
  NOT PRODUCIBLE.
- The G1.1 Replay design is unchanged.

## J. Accessibility (brief §30)

**Verified in Chrome:**

- **Controls:** every primary control is named, ≥ 44 × 44, and shows its state by shape (**G12-Q11b**).
- **Words:** every call phase has words in both languages, readable in full at 100% and 200% text (**G12-Q11a**).
- **Keyboard:** Escape unwinds a layer and never ends a call (**G12-Q11c**).
- **Reduced Motion:** reaches the same state, controls and words in all six journeys (**G12-M1**).
- **Contrast and text size:** Increase Contrast keeps the hierarchy (`a11y-01/02`); 200% text keeps every control
  (`a11y-03/04/05`).
- **Mixed script:** Arabic / Latin mixed content stays correct (`bi-01/02`; **G12-BI**).
- **Denial:** microphone denial leaves Writing (**G12-P1**).
- **Without audio:** a reader who cannot hear has the words, the time and every control to navigate, end and recover.
- **OS surfaces:** acknowledged, never replaced by custom UI.

**Defects this task found and fixed** (design-critique pass):

1. The call's words were **clipped with an ellipsis** at 200% text and for V06 at 100%. They now stand in full in a
   strip above the line whenever the line cannot hold them.
2. The speaker route was visible only in the accessible name. It is now a toggle whose glyph changes shape.
3. The OS prompt's Arabic purpose string was bidi-broken inside an LTR schematic. It is now its own RTL paragraph.

**Not verified:** VoiceOver and TalkBack on real devices; Dynamic Type / Android font scale on device; the OS call UI's
own accessibility. Production note: `useReducedMotion` in Reanimated reads the setting **at app start** only, so a
minutes-long call needs `ReduceMotion.System` per animation.
