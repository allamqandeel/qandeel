# C3 FINAL DESIGN DIRECTOR CLOSURE RECORD

**Stage:** I-08B3.1-C — Living Brass Material System

**Independent review result:** APPROVED / VERIFIED / CLOSED / FROZEN

This record is additive. It does not rewrite the executor's pre-freeze `C3_FREEZE_RECORD.md`; it records the Design Director decision made after independent review.

## Canonical Living Brass freeze

- Dark-expression Living Brass body: `#A58E6F`.
- Material at ordinary/small scale: **QUIET SATIN**.
- Rare large identity expression: restrained **BRUSHED / HANDLED** character permitted.
- Coverage policy: **P2 — IDENTITY MACHINERY FAMILY**.
- Persistent QANDEEL navigation / identity machinery: Living Brass permitted and expected as one coherent material family.
- Living Brass itself is **state-invariant**; selection, focus, press, disabled and status remain independently expressed by later systems.
- Analytical Map marks do not use Brass as analytical encoding.
- Brass does not encode status, confidence, evidence, importance, recency, FAR/MID/NEAR or premium entitlement.
- Luxury risk is controlled by application discipline, not by weakening the material.
- Character permission is governed by **permission class, not an arbitrary size threshold**. The proof-era `96 px` constant is not canonical.

## Explicitly not frozen by C

QANDEEL Light; Light-appearance Brass; interaction/status/focus/disabled expressions; final tab/navigation geometry, selected-state treatment, labels, motion, transitions and active indicator; final icon geometry; lantern animation; Q-thread expression; final accessibility transformations.

## Visual-vitality authority

The C freeze does **not** close QANDEEL's visual identity. The neutral World remains a stage for later Light, motion, spatial behavior, distinctive navigation form, typography and authored interaction. The standing **Life Test** remains active: a technically correct screen that feels interchangeable with a competent grey utility app is incomplete.

## Review-note correction

The submitted package had one packaging defect: `data/C3_MANIFEST.json` included a hash of itself. A self-referential manifest cannot remain stable after serialization. The final closure package excludes both manifest outputs from the hashed file set. No Product token, source value, raster, accepted composition, or design decision changed.
