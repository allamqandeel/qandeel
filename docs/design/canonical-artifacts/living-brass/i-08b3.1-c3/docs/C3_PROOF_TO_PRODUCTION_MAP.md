# C3_PROOF_TO_PRODUCTION_MAP

**I-08B3.1-C3.9.** What survives into production, what was proof-only, and who owns what is left.

The purpose of this document is that **nothing accidentally inherits authority it was never given**.
A proof harness produces a great many artefacts, and the ones that are load-bearing and the ones that
were scaffolding are indistinguishable from the outside.

### 4.1 The disabled collision — a dependency, not an oversight

C1R recorded that the disabled neutral sits close enough to the rest-state ink to be argued with, and
that the collision worsens as the body gets stronger. **`#A58E6F` is the strongest body tested.** C2
was instructed not to solve it; C3 does not solve it either.

Handed to E, intact, rather than quietly fixed and lost from the record.

### 4.2 The persistent mark — an open question C3 cannot close

Current Apple guidance says: *"Resist the temptation to display your logo throughout your app or game
unless it's essential for providing context."*

C2 flagged that the canonical Q stands on every screen under **both** coverage policies, so choosing
between them could not resolve it. **C3 cannot resolve it either**, because it is a PLACEMENT question
and `qandeel.identity.mark` grants MATERIAL. The token says what the mark is made of if it is there.

## 5. The one reclassification C3 makes

Everything else in this document carries a classification forward from C1R or C2. This is the single
change:

| | C1R / C2 | **C3** |
|---|---|---|
| `GRAIN_MIN_PX = 96` | the rule that keeps the character off ordinary marks | **`character.scale.proofEraConstant` — recorded, not frozen** |
| the rule that replaces it | — | **`permission-not-size`** |

**Why.** C3 measured the character at fifteen widths and found it delivers 82–93 % of its accepted
amplitude at every one, navigation size included. There is no knee. The inherited number was never a
capability limit; it was a restraint expressed as a number, and freezing it would have stated a design
decision in the grammar of physics.

**What it changes in the accepted evidence: nothing.** C3.12 rebuilt all nine accepted compositions
with the permission rule in place of the size rule and every raster came back byte-identical. The two
rules agree everywhere anyone has looked; they differ only in what they would permit somewhere nobody
has been.

**What it changes in the contract: the burden.** Under a size rule, small marks are safe
automatically. Under a permission rule, they are safe because someone decided. The uncomfortable half
is recorded in `C3_CANONICAL_LIVING_BRASS_SPEC.md` §4.4: **the character works at navigation size, so
nothing physical stops it going there, and it would look good.**
