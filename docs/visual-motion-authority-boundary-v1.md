---

## 1. Why this document exists

T-10 froze a truth-preserving motion system and refused a long list of motion behaviours. Two entries
on that list name a **noun** rather than a behaviour — `permanent glow or neon` and `particle field` —
and a noun can be read as a ban on the material itself rather than on the way it was proposed to move.

That reading is too broad, and it is the wrong reading. Left standing it would let a future task cite
a motion contract as the reason QANDEEL may not have light, depth or texture — which is an authority
T-10 was never given and never claimed.

This document states the boundary. It changes no motion decision, no duration, no easing, no token, no
camera or disclosure choreography, no accessibility or reduced-motion behaviour, and no Product
semantics. It authorizes nothing new. It says which owner answers which question.

---

## 2. The authority statement

> **T-10 governs motion semantics and dynamic response. It does not govern the Static/Graphic Visual
> Language.**
>
> **A visual treatment is not forbidden merely because it glows, carries particulate or filamentary
> texture, or creates atmospheric depth. A motion treatment is not permitted merely because the
> underlying visual material is permitted.**
>
> **The two authorities meet at truth. Neither erases the other.**

### 2.1 What T-10 owns

Motion semantics · dynamic response · animation causality · temporal and spatial continuity expressed
by movement · interruptibility · reduced-motion parity · and the whole question of whether movement is
allowed to imply a Product fact.

### 2.2 What T-10 does not own

What the world is made of when it is standing still.

## 3. The rejections that remain frozen

Every one of these remains refused, as a **dynamic motion behaviour**, exactly as T-10 §11 refuses it.
Nothing in this document re-authorizes any of them, and only an explicitly authorized future motion
contract can:

Field Resonance · neighbour ripple · ambient breathing · velocity-based node sizing · arrival breath ·
motion-driven field breathing · animated tether tension · bouncing nodes · rubber-band world ·
infinite repeat · breathing Live indicator · decorative motion that makes neighbouring objects react
to a gesture without Product authority · motion that visually asserts confidence, importance, recency,
causality, relation, temporal availability or semantic change without authoritative runtime truth.

T-10 §11's full list is the authority. The set above restates its dynamic half so that this document
can never be read as narrowing it; where the two are read together, §11 governs.

---

## 4. The material this list does not ban

`permanent glow or neon` and `particle field` are motion-vocabulary rejections. They are not a
prohibition on non-motion visual material, and they must not be read as one. The following are legal
**as visual and material language**:

- restrained persistent luminance;
- low-energy luminous halos;
- atmospheric glow and falloff;
- luminous semantic territories;
- non-semantic dust and particulate texture;
- filamentary light texture;
- sparse points of light;
- atmospheric depth and noise;
- static or materially persistent field texture;
- restrained Living Brass illumination;
- colour atmosphere belonging to an already-authorized object or territory;
- non-semantic visual substrate that helps the Living Analysis Map read as organic, spatial and alive.

They do not become Product facts merely because they are visible.

---

## 5. The gate is truth, not restraint

Visual material is governed by the rule that already governs everything QANDEEL draws:

> **A visual relationship may be drawn only if it is true of the runtime.**

So atmosphere and material may **not**:

- invent a relationship between objects;
- let a line or filament imply a relation the runtime does not support;
- use intensity to imply confidence, importance or recency unless explicitly authorized;
- use direction or flow to imply influence or causality without authority;
- reveal information that is not yet available;
- ghost historical or future objects absent from the current disclosed `V`;
- manufacture hierarchy or rank;
- manufacture a semantic event;
- stand in for actual Product state.

Richness is legal. False claims are not. A treatment is judged by what it *asserts*, never by how much
light it carries — which is why "make it plainer" is not an answer to a truth defect, and "it is only
decoration" is not a defence of one.

---

## 6. Motion over that material — the one-way rule

**QAN-GOV-02 authorizes no new motion.**

Material that is legal while still does not thereby become legal to move. The rule runs one way only,
and this is the half that keeps T-10 whole: legality at rest is a visual-truth judgement; legality in
motion is a motion-truth judgement, and it is a separate judgement made by the motion owner under the
applicable motion contract.

Any future proposal to animate particles, atmospheric filaments, halos, field texture or luminous
substrate is a motion proposal and is answered there. Until such a contract explicitly authorizes it,
this document must never be cited to add:

ambient breathing · particle drift · neighbour reaction · tether tension · Field Resonance ·
infinite ambient loops · gesture-reactive decorative physics.

## 7. The executable contract is unchanged

`tests/t10-motion-contract.test.mjs` is untouched by QAN-GOV-02 and still refuses every pattern in
§11. Its scans read **comment-stripped source identifiers inside the owner's own code** — the
`particle`-class token scan over the motion owner, the Map and the Timeline's motion module; and a
narrower word scan (`ring`, `bloom`, `ripple`, `glow`, `brass`, `ignition`, `pulse`, `parallax`,
`halo`) over the motion owner alone. Nothing in that gate inspects rendered output, and nothing in it
inspects documentation.

A future visual implementation is therefore expected to satisfy that gate **unchanged**. The gate
constrains what the motion owner may name and reach; it is not, and was never, a statement about what
the reader is allowed to see. Re-anchoring or weakening it to make room for a visual treatment is not
authorized by this document — an implementation that believes it needs that has found a motion
question, not a visual one, and owes it to the motion owner.

## 8. Visual ambition

T-10 may not be cited as a reason to flatten QANDEEL into plain circles on a dark background, a
generic node-graph UI, sterile cards, low-character dashboard visuals, or a motion-safe but
aesthetically impoverished map.

The legal design space still includes, where truthful: dark spatial depth · organic luminous
territories · differentiated colour atmospheres · layered light falloff · filamentary and dust
texture · restrained persistent halos · fine spatial detail · Living Brass accents · premium material
depth · a coherent semantic world rather than a collection of cards.

This is permission for **visual craft**. It is not permission to invent semantics, and it makes no
image pixel-canonical.

### The Stage 4.2 proof-board

The Stage 4.2 Semantic Zoom Disclosure proof-board is a **visual ambition reference**, not a
production contract, and not a repository document — it belongs to the out-of-repo Living Analysis Map
exploration track. QAN-GOV-02 protects the ability to realize its relevant visual qualities where they
are compatible with current frozen Product truth: luminous semantic regions, atmospheric depth, rich
particulate and filament texture, coloured field identity, restrained brass light, organic spatial
composition.

It authorizes none of: proof-board labels, legends or tables as Product UI; false relation lines;
motion behaviours already rejected by T-10; or any semantic claim the runtime does not support.

## 9. The permanent interpretation rule

Future task contracts and reviews apply this distinction:

> **Static/material richness is governed by visual truth. Dynamic response is governed by motion
> truth.**

Two failure modes it exists to prevent, in both directions:

| A reviewer says | The correct answer |
| --- | --- |
| "T-10 rejected `particle field`, so this texture is out." | §11 rejects the *behaviour*. Ask instead what the texture claims, and judge that against §5. |
| "The halo is already approved, so animating it is approved." | §6. Legality at rest is not legality in motion. Ask the motion owner. |

Neither owner may answer the other's question. Where a treatment is both material and moving, it needs
both answers, and the absence of either is a rejection.
