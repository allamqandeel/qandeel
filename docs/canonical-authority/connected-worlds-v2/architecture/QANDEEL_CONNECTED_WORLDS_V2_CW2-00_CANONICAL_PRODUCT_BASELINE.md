`MY_WORLD`

`SHARED_WORLD`

`PUBLIC_WORLD`

`REPLAY`

`INTRODUCTIONS / MATCHING`

This preserves a coherent product universe without creating separate mini-applications inside QANDEEL.

---

# 4. MY_WORLD

The Personal World remains the existing frozen Living Analysis Map.

Connected Worlds v2 must preserve its established truths, including:

- persistent semantic world;
- stable semantic geography;
- semantic zoom as disclosure;
- temporal truth;
- no hindsight leakage;
- truthful provenance;
- truth-preserving motion;
- visual relationships only when runtime-true;
- existing Living Analysis Map navigation semantics.

Connected Worlds may extend the system around `MY_WORLD`, but must not silently weaken or reinterpret its frozen behavior.

---

# 5. SHARED_WORLD

A Shared World is a persistent world belonging collectively to its human participants with QANDEEL present.

Its primary v1 experience is optimized for:

`2 humans + QANDEEL`

but the product concept remains extensible to a small number of additional participants.

A Shared World:

- is born only after explicit acceptance;
- has its own truth, history, analysis and timeline;
- is not a Group Chat;
- does not expose participants' private worlds;
- may use private knowledge only under the agreed permission model;
- supports text and voice notes;
- anticipates human-to-human live calling architecturally but does not require it in v1;
- may contain Replay;
- preserves user authority over their own contributed material;
- supports voluntary exit, removal by the agreed governance model, rejoin rules and world closure;
- can show other Shared Worlds only as privacy-safe existence-level presences where product design permits.

---

# 6. PUBLIC_WORLD

The Public World is a single large semantic world of intentionally published Experiences.

It is not a chronological social feed.

Its product principles include:

- world-first exploration;
- semantic-field geography;
- spatial proximity means semantic similarity;
- explicit lines mean real recorded relationships;
- stable Experience position;
- activity changes visual vitality, not semantic geography;
- old content cools rather than disappears;
- text and voice discussion;
- `@qandeel` public participation using public context only;
- no direct messaging to Experience owners;
- pseudonymous public identity by default;
- user-authorized publication of conversation, call, QANDEEL analysis and Replay;
- complete public disappearance when an owner deletes their Experience.

---

# 7. REPLAY

Replay is a cross-world capability.

Core product truth:

> **الصوت = ما حدث.**
>
> **الصورة = كيف فهمه قنديل.**

Replay:

- preserves the original medium;
- preserves temporal truth;
- does not invent speech, events or hindsight;
- may be created from Private, Shared or owned Public material;
- supports natural-language selection and simple editing;
- supports trimming, selecting and connecting real segments in truthful chronological order;
- lets QANDEEL perform simple transitions automatically;
- does not become a full video editor in v1;
- supports Public World, external sharing and download destinations;
- remains governed by the rights of the underlying material.

Live in-conversation Replay capture is deferred beyond v1.

---

# 8. INTRODUCTIONS / MATCHING

Matching v1 is:

> **Serious Relationship / Marriage Introductions**

It is a doorway mechanism hosted from `MY_WORLD`, not a fourth world or separate Dating App.

Core product truths include:

- dual entry: natural conversational discovery + quiet manual entry;
- explicit user activation;
- private matching status;
- no KYC or mandatory identity/age proof as a product prerequisite;
- explicit permission to use the user's self-authored private knowledge internally for matching;
- a small `Introduction Profile` controls what may be disclosed;
- first name only before Match;
- no photos before Mutual Match;
- about five proposals per week as an initial product value;
- no swipe deck;
- no public candidate marketplace;
- no visible compatibility score or ranking;
- safe compatibility explanation without revealing private evidence;
- hard dealbreakers are never overridden;
- multiple bounded pending proposals may exist before a Match;
- first Mutual Match closes the other live proposals;
- one active Introduction at a time;
- Mutual Match creates a `SHARED_WORLD` beginning in `Introduction Phase`;
- QANDEEL actively breaks the ice and guides early interaction;
- progressive disclosure remains under each person's independent control;
- QANDEEL may suggest disclosure but never performs it automatically;
- matching permission does not automatically transfer into Shared World permission;
- transition from `Introduction Phase` to normal Shared World requires QANDEEL suggestion + both participants' approval;
- either participant may end the Introduction immediately;
- a failed Introduction becomes read-only historical truth;
- successful transition to normal Shared World ends that Matching cycle and pauses new marriage proposals until the user deliberately reactivates them.

---

# 9. Core Cross-World Principle

The new architecture must enforce:

> **Knowledge possession is not audience permission.**

QANDEEL may know something in one context without being allowed to expose or use it in another.

Every cross-world use of material or private knowledge must have an explicit authority basis.

No capability may silently widen an audience.

---

# 10. Clean Architecture Rule

Connected Worlds v2 must be additive and compositional.

The architecture must prefer:

> **نزود، ما نمسحش.**
>
> **نوسع، ما نغيرش معنى القديم.**
>
> **نركب الجديد فوق الموجود، ما نخلطش الحاجات ببعض.**

Any required change to a frozen Personal World truth must be explicit, versioned and justified by a backward-equivalence/non-regression proof.
