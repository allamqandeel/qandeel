# A. THREAD LIFECYCLE CONTRACT

## A1. Mention / Entity is pre-Thread evidence, not a Thread state

A person, topic, place, organization or other referent may occur repeatedly in committed CUs without becoming a Conversation Thread.

Example:

> «المدير طلب التقرير، وأحمد بعته، وبعدها أحمد كلمني عشان نسخة تانية.»

`Ahmed` is present and may be perfectly resolved.

That proves:

**Ahmed exists as a conversational referent.**

It does not yet prove:

**Ahmed is an independently addressable locus of conversational attention.**

Therefore:

> **Reference continuity is necessary for some Thread decisions, but reference continuity alone is never Thread establishment.**

---

## A2. Emerging Focus

An **Emerging Focus** exists when committed conversational material begins to treat some sufficiently identifiable subject/context as an independent target of attention, but there is not yet enough evidence to justify permanent Thread identity.

It is:

- provisional;
- non-persistent as Thread geography;
- allowed to disappear;
- allowed to fail promotion;
- grounded in conversation rather than model curiosity.

### Minimum requirement

At least one committed CU must provide a genuine **independent-attention signal**.

A QANDEEL Reading, Unknown or internal analytical interest alone cannot create Emerging Focus from nothing.

---

## A3. Established Thread

An Emerging Focus becomes an **Established Thread** when the conversation demonstrates both:

### Independent conversational attention

and

### User-addressable significance

strongly enough that QANDEEL can truthfully preserve the focus as something the conversation may later return to.

Establishment creates persistent canonical Thread identity.

Once Established, the Thread never returns to “Mention” or “Emerging.”

It may subsequently be:

- Active;
- Dormant;
- Reopened.

---

## A4. Active

An Established Thread is **Active** while committed conversational attention is currently centered on it or an ongoing conversational sequence remains meaningfully anchored to it.

Active does not mean:

- important;
- high confidence;
- dominant;
- emotionally significant.

It means only:

> **currently receiving conversational attention.**

---

## A5. Dormant

An Established Thread becomes **Dormant** when conversational attention has stably moved away from it.

Dormant means:

> **not the current conversational locus, while canonical identity and history remain intact.**

Dormancy is not:

- deletion;
- closure;
- rejection;
- irrelevance;
- low importance.

---

## A6. Reopened

A Dormant Thread becomes **Reopened** when a new committed conversational act directly returns attention to the same sufficiently resolved Thread.

Reopened:

- retains the original Thread identity;
- does not require re-establishment;
- records genuine return after dormancy;
- may then continue as Active.

---

# B. ESTABLISHMENT / PROMOTION EVIDENCE CONTRACT

## B1. No generic Thread score

Stage 1.3 does **not** define:

- Thread probability;
- promotion percentage;
- importance score;
- weighted keyword score.

Promotion is based on grounded conversational evidence.

The key question is:

> **Has this become something the conversation is independently about, rather than merely something mentioned while talking about something else?**

---

# B2. Four required evidence dimensions

For an Established Thread, the grammar must be able to defend four things.

## 1. Identity / Focus Grounding

QANDEEL must know sufficiently what the proposed focus refers to.

This may come from:

- explicit naming;
- resolved pronoun continuity;
- explicit clarification;
- stable conversational description;
- a relational focus whose participants are sufficiently resolved.

Ambiguous identity weakens or blocks promotion.

---

## 2. Independent Attention

The proposed focus must become a target of conversational action in its own right.

Evidence may include CUs that:

- ask directly about it;
- request discussion of it;
- elaborate on it;
- challenge something about it;
- clarify it;
- correct understanding of it;
- explicitly redirect attention toward it;
- provide substantive information primarily about it.

### Example

> «وأحمد كان في الاجتماع.»

Ahmed = incidental participant.

Versus:

> «بس أحمد نفسه هو اللي مقلقني.»

Now Ahmed is the target of the contribution.

That is independent-attention evidence.

---

## 3. User-Addressable Significance

Permanent Thread identity requires evidence that the subject is meaningfully addressable by the user, rather than merely something QANDEEL chose to analyze.

Strong evidence includes:

> «خلينا نتكلم عن أحمد.»

> «عايز أرجع لأحمد.»

> «المشكلة عندي مع أحمد نفسه.»

or substantive user participation in a sequence explicitly centered on Ahmed.

The user does not have to originate every focus.

QANDEEL may ask:

> «إيه اللي حصل بينك وبين أحمد؟»

and the user's substantive answer may establish that Ahmed is genuinely user-addressable.

But QANDEEL repeatedly asking about Ahmed while the user does not engage cannot establish the Thread by itself.

---

## 4. Persistence / Conversational Substance

Unless establishment is explicit, attention must survive beyond an incidental isolated mention.

This may be demonstrated through:

- continued discussion across several committed CUs;
- a question→answer sequence centered on the focus;
- elaboration or clarification around it;
- recurrence after intervening material;
- repeated substantive user return.

Duration alone is irrelevant.

Ten seconds of directly focused discussion may establish more strongly than ten minutes containing repeated incidental mentions.

---

# B3. Three legitimate promotion pathways

No single pathway is mandatory.

## Path A — Explicit Selection

One sufficiently clear committed user CU may establish immediately.

Example:

> «بص، أنا عايز أتكلم معاك عن أحمد تحديدًا.»

If `Ahmed` is sufficiently resolved:

- independent attention = explicit;
- user-addressability = explicit;
- substantive conversational intent = explicit.

No artificial requirement for multiple mentions exists.

---

## Path B — Sustained Engagement

Without explicit selection, a focus may establish through a sustained conversational sequence.

Example:

User:

> «بس أحمد نفسه مضايقني.»

QANDEEL:

> «إيه اللي مضايقك منه؟»

User:

> «بقاله فترة بيتعامل معايا بطريقة غريبة...»

The sequence shows:

- stable referent;
- direct targeting;
- continued user engagement;
- independent conversational substance.

Ahmed may now be Established.

---

## Path C — Recurring Independent Attention

A focus may be briefly independent more than once without establishing on its first appearance.

Example:

Earlier:

> «على فكرة أحمد عمل حاجة غريبة، بس نكمل موضوع الشغل الأول.»

Later:

> «نرجع لأحمد بقى، عشان اللي عمله مهم.»

The first episode may create Emerging Focus.

The later explicit return demonstrates recurrence and user-addressability strongly enough for promotion.

The earlier source material can contribute as evidence because it remains committed conversational history.

No permanent Thread identity existed before actual promotion.

---

# B4. Supporting signals that are not sufficient alone

The following can strengthen a focus but cannot establish it by themselves:

- repeated lexical mentions;
- repeated resolved pronouns;
- entity frequency;
- one factual statement containing the entity;
- co-occurrence with an existing Thread;
- QANDEEL's analytical interest;
- a QANDEEL-generated Reading;
- an Unknown;
- a QANDEEL Question that the user does not meaningfully engage with;
- emotional language without independent attention;
- the subject appearing in Evidence;
- semantic similarity to another Thread.

---

# B5. Correctly resolved references support continuity

Once a focus is identifiable, later CUs do not need to repeat its name.

Example:

> «أحمد مضايقني.»

then:

> «هو بقاله أسبوع بيتجنبني.»

If `هو → Ahmed` is `RESOLVED`, the second CU may contribute to the same focus.

Therefore:

> **Thread continuity follows grounded reference identity, not lexical repetition.**

---

# B6. Ambiguous references constrain promotion

Example:

> «أحمد وخالد بقوا غريبين الفترة دي.»

Later:

> «هو اللي مضايقني أكتر.»

If:

`هو → AMBIGUOUS {Ahmed, Khaled}`

the second CU cannot be counted as identity-specific independent-attention evidence for Ahmed or Khaled.

QANDEEL may know:

> someone among those candidates is receiving attention.

It may not claim:

> Ahmed is independently receiving that attention.

Ambiguity may therefore:

- leave focus unpromoted;
- delay promotion;
- require clarification.

It must never be resolved merely because promotion would make the model cleaner.

---

# B7. Unresolved reference cannot establish a specific Thread

Example:

> «هو بقى المشكلة الحقيقية.»

with no defensible antecedent.

This may contain independent conversational attention toward some referent.

But it cannot establish:

**Ahmed Thread**

or another specific Thread.

Unknown identity and meaningful attention are different facts.

---

# B8. Attribution matters to Thread evidence

Reported speech must retain its Stage 1.2 attribution.

Example:

> «خالد قعد يقوللي: أحمد هو سبب المشكلة، أحمد لازم يمشي، أحمد بوظ الدنيا.»

Those three quoted mentions do not mean the **user** has independently centered Ahmed.

They prove that the user reports Khaled as repeatedly talking about Ahmed.

The surrounding user conversation may later independently center Ahmed—but quotation frequency alone cannot establish it.

---

# B9. Analysis may strengthen Emerging Focus, never establish permanent Thread alone

Suppose committed conversation creates an Emerging Focus around Ahmed.

QANDEEL then forms:

- a Reading concerning Ahmed;
- an Unknown involving Ahmed;
- a Question intended to clarify Ahmed's role.

Those objects may provide analytical reason to continue investigating the Emerging Focus.

But if the conversation never provides sufficient independent/user-addressable attention:

> **Ahmed remains unestablished.**

Especially:

QANDEEL must not reason:

> “I now have three interesting analytical objects about Ahmed, therefore Ahmed deserves a Thread.”

That would violate Stage 0.1.

---

# C. DORMANT / REOPENED CONTRACT

## C1. Dormancy follows stable attention shift, not a timer

No rule such as:

> “not mentioned for 30 seconds → Dormant”

is valid.

A Thread becomes Dormant when the conversational sequence demonstrates that another locus has stably taken current attention.

Evidence may include:

- explicit `FOCUS_SHIFT`;
- sustained discussion elsewhere;
- completion of the local sequence followed by different material.

---

# C2. Brief interruption does not necessarily cause Dormancy

Ahmed is Active.

QANDEEL asks:

> «استنى بس، حصل ده إمتى؟»

User:

> «الثلاث اللي فات.»

The conversation has not left Ahmed merely because a temporal clarification occurred.

The clarification remains locally anchored to Ahmed.

Likewise:

> «ثانية أرد على الباب...»

does not create a new conversational focus.

Dormancy requires actual conversational departure, not every interruption.

---

# C3. Explicit departure is strong Dormancy evidence

Example:

> «سيبك من أحمد دلوقتي، خلينا نرجع للشغل.»

Functions include:

- `REQUEST`
- `FOCUS_SHIFT`

If the new sequence proceeds around Work:

Ahmed → **Dormant**

The Thread persists.

---

# C4. Reopening requires the same grounded Thread identity

Later:

> «نرجع لأحمد بقى.»

If `Ahmed` resolves to the original Ahmed Thread:

Ahmed → **Reopened**

No new Ahmed Thread is created.

No re-promotion threshold is required.

---

# C5. Pronoun-based reopening is allowed when resolution is strong

Later:

> «طب نرجعله تاني.»

If the conversational context uniquely resolves:

`ـه → Ahmed`

the existing Ahmed Thread may be Reopened.

If the pronoun is ambiguous:

no identity-specific reopening may be asserted.

---

# C6. QANDEEL may reopen an already-established Thread conversationally

For an existing Dormant Thread, either participant may explicitly return to it.

Example:

QANDEEL:

> «قبل ما نكمل، عايز أرجع لحاجة قلتها عن أحمد.»

This is a committed conversational return to an already-existing Thread.

It may reopen Ahmed.

This is different from QANDEEL analysis **establishing** a new Thread.

The establishment prohibition does not mean QANDEEL can never conversationally return to already-established material.

---

# C7. Analytical resurfacing alone does not reopen conversational focus

If an internal Reading concerning dormant Ahmed is updated while the live conversation remains about Work:

Ahmed does not become Reopened merely because background analysis touched it.

Thread activity is conversational.

Analytical activity is not conversational focus.

---

# D. REFINEMENT VS REFRAMING CONTRACT

## D1. Refinement preserves the same conversational locus

A label can improve when later material disambiguates or describes the same focus more accurately.

Example:

Initial:

**Ahmed**

Later clarification:

> «أحمد زميلي في فرع أكتوبر، مش أحمد أخويا.»

Possible refined label:

**Ahmed — work colleague**

Same referent.

Same conversational locus.

Same Thread identity.

This is refinement.

---

# D2. Additional properties do not automatically create new Threads

Ahmed Thread:

> «أحمد زميلي.»

> «أحمد عنده 35 سنة.»

> «أحمد شغال في المبيعات.»

Those are additional propositions about Ahmed.

They do not each become:

- Ahmed's job Thread;
- Ahmed's age Thread;
- Ahmed's department Thread.

The conversation must independently address the new focus.

---

# D3. Reframing creates a new Thread when the new focus becomes independently addressable

Consider:

**Ahmed**

versus:

**My relationship with Ahmed**

They are not ontologically interchangeable.

The relational focus includes Ahmed but is about:

> **the relationship between the user and Ahmed**

rather than Ahmed alone.

A relational Thread may emerge when conversation begins treating that relationship itself as an independent subject.

Example:

> «المشكلة مش أحمد كشخص أصلًا... أنا حاسس إن علاقتنا إحنا الاتنين بقت متوترة.»

This is evidence for a new relational Emerging Focus.

Later:

> «خلينا نتكلم في علاقتي بأحمد نفسها.»

may establish:

**Relationship with Ahmed**

without changing or renaming:

**Ahmed**

---

# D4. Independence test for Reframing

A proposed new focus should become a separate Thread only if both can remain meaningfully addressable.

Ask conceptually:

> Can the conversation return to Ahmed-the-person without necessarily discussing the relationship?

and:

> Can it return specifically to the relationship with Ahmed without discussing everything about Ahmed?

If yes, and conversational evidence establishes both, they are separate Threads.

---

# D5. Mere relational language is insufficient

> «أحمد زميلي.»

contains a relationship.

It does not create **Relationship with Ahmed** Thread.

Likewise:

> «أنا اتخانقت مع أحمد امبارح.»

contains an interpersonal event.

It may remain material inside Ahmed or another current focus.

A relational Thread requires independent conversational attention to the relationship as such.

---

# D6. New Thread does not rewrite old understanding

If Relationship with Ahmed later becomes Established:

Ahmed remains Ahmed.

Historical CUs previously grounded in Ahmed are not silently reclassified as though the relational Thread had always existed.

Later stages may define participation/relations between them.

Stage 1.3 does not.

---

## Step 2 — Ahmed appears only as a mention

User:

> «المدير كل يوم بيغير المطلوب، وامبارح أحمد اضطر يعيد التقرير مرتين.»

Ahmed is correctly identified as a colleague from context.

But the CU is primarily about the Work situation.

**Ahmed → MENTION ONLY**

No Emerging Focus yet.

Repeated occurrence inside a Work narrative is insufficient.

---

## Step 3 — More mention still does not establish

User:

> «وبعدها أحمد كلمني وقاللي المدير كان متعصب طول اليوم.»

Conversational speaker = USER.

**Ahmed → MENTION ONLY**

Two mentions did not create a Thread.

---

## Step 4 — Ahmed gains independent attention

User:

> «بس عارف؟ أحمد نفسه بدأ يقلقني أكتر من المدير.»

Committed CU:

- `INFORM_REPORT`
- effectively redirects attention toward Ahmed;
- Ahmed reference is resolved.

Ahmed is now being predicated about **as the subject of concern itself**.

**Ahmed → EMERGING FOCUS**

Not yet necessarily Established.

---

## Step 5 — QANDEEL strengthens the focus conversationally

QANDEEL:

> «إيه اللي بدأ يقلقك في أحمد؟»

Committed CU:

- `ASK`
- target = Ahmed Emerging Focus.

The question strengthens conversational attention.

But if the user now said:

> «لا سيبك منه، مش مهم.»

Ahmed need not establish.

QANDEEL's question alone is insufficient.

---

## Step 6 — User supplies sustained independent attention

User:

> «بقاله شهر تقريبًا كل ما أحكيله حاجة عن المدير ألاقيها وصلت له بعدها. ومش عارف ده صدفة ولا هو بيحكي له.»

Committed CUs concern Ahmed directly.

Resolved:

> «ـه / هو → Ahmed»

User provides sustained substantive engagement.

We now have:

- stable identity;
- independent attention;
- user-addressability;
- sustained conversational substance.

**Ahmed → ESTABLISHED / ACTIVE**

Work may now become Dormant because attention has stably shifted away.

---

## Step 7 — Relationship with Ahmed begins emerging separately

User:

> «بس يمكن المشكلة مش أحمد كشخص. أنا حاسس إن علاقتنا إحنا الاتنين اتغيرت جدًا.»

`علاقتنا` resolves through the local user↔Ahmed context.

This is no longer simply another fact about Ahmed.

The relationship itself has become the target.

**Relationship with Ahmed → EMERGING FOCUS**

**Ahmed Thread remains Ahmed.**

---

## Step 8 — Relational focus becomes independently addressable

QANDEEL:

> «التغيير في علاقتكم باين لك في إيه؟»

User:

> «في الثقة أكتر حاجة. خلينا نتكلم في علاقتي بأحمد نفسها، لأن حتى بره موضوع الشغل بقينا بعيد عن بعض.»

The user explicitly selects the relationship as subject and distinguishes it from Work.

**Relationship with Ahmed → ESTABLISHED / ACTIVE**

Ahmed remains an existing Thread.

Nothing has been merged or renamed.

---

## Step 9 — Conversation returns to Work

User:

> «بس نسيب علاقتي بأحمد دلوقتي ونرجع للشغل نفسه، أنا لازم أقرر هكمل ولا أمشي.»

Committed CU:

- `REQUEST`
- `FOCUS_SHIFT`

**Relationship with Ahmed → DORMANT**

**Ahmed → DORMANT**

**Work → REOPENED**

Same original Work Thread.

---

## Step 10 — Work continues

Conversation remains centered on:

- workload;
- manager;
- whether to leave.

Ahmed may still be mentioned incidentally:

> «وأحمد قاللي السوق برا الشركة أحسن.»

That mention does not reopen Ahmed automatically if it merely serves the Work discussion.

**Ahmed remains DORMANT.**

---

## Step 11 — Conversation explicitly returns to Ahmed

Later user:

> «على فكرة، قبل ما نقفل موضوع الشغل عايز أرجع لأحمد نفسه.»

Resolved Ahmed identity = original work colleague.

Explicit user return.

**Ahmed → REOPENED**

Not:

**Ahmed-2**

Not:

new Thread.

The same canonical Ahmed Thread is active again with another temporal episode.
