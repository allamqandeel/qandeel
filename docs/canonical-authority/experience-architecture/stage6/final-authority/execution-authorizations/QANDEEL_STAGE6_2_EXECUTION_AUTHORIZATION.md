- ordinal;

## FOLLOW_LIVE

- visible Track semantics;

Stage 5.3 explicitly left navigation-coordinate/mapping mechanics open; do not assume that a visually non-metric region cannot support temporal targeting.

The final contract must make clear what is:
- user-visible semantic geometry;
- interaction mapping;
- derived hidden calculation.

# 2. EXECUTION-GATE EG-02
## Do not assume a conventional slider/scrubber accessibility topology

Research must explicitly test whether common range-widget semantics are compatible with Stage 5's epistemic firewall.

A conventional accessible range control may expose:
- minimum;
- maximum;
- current numeric value;
- relative position;
- increment/step semantics.

If those values reveal future-relative extent, count or position while `PINNED`, that topology is invalid for the relevant state.

Therefore:

> **Accessibility equivalence is frozen; conventional slider semantics are not.**

Stage 6.2 may recommend a topology/interaction requirement for its Timeline decisions, but:

- do not freeze a web-specific ARIA implementation;
- do not assume one widget role works in every temporal mode;
- do not enumerate future Moments;
- do not expose future set-size/count metadata;
- do not revive OPEN-08 coarse stepping merely to solve accessibility.

If research indicates a conventional pattern is incompatible, report the constraint and propose QANDEEL-specific Experience semantics while leaving platform implementation mechanics to 6.4/6.5.

Frozen semantics require:

```text
Moment(LH) ≠ LIVE_EDGE
```

The final Experience must let a user intentionally invoke either meaning when they are co-located.

But Stage 6.2 must not assume in advance that this requires:
- two permanent buttons;

The decision must specify:

1. how the two intents are discoverable;
2. how the active temporal mode is legible;
3. how the distinction is operable by touch/pointer;
4. how equivalent intent is available nonvisually;
5. what happens when Live advances;
6. how returning to Live differs from selecting the latest absolute Moment;
7. what no-hindsight metadata is forbidden.

The semantic outcome must be deterministic independent of final decoration.

# 8. OPEN-13 Decision Framework

Compare at least three viable policy families unless research proves one invalid before comparison.

At minimum evaluate:

## Option family A — Ordinal / committed-position scale

Potential premise:
visible distance primarily expresses committed conversational order rather than elapsed duration.

Attack:
- long pauses;
- dense bursts;
- perception of equal importance;
- relationship to aggregation;
- mobile density;
- historical post-TC firewall.

## Option family B — Duration-proportional scale

Potential premise:
visible distance expresses elapsed current-session time.

Attack:
- long silence;
- tiny dense interactions;
- storage/timestamp dependency;
- touch target usability;
- accessibility value semantics;
- pinned future-extent leakage.

## Option family C — Adaptive / hybrid scale

Potential premise:
preserve some temporal duration information while maintaining usable density.

Attack:
- whether changing scale silently changes semantic meaning;
- whether the adaptation itself communicates importance/density;
- determinism;
- spatial memory;
- implementation/test complexity;
- no-hindsight behavior.

Other options may be added, but arbitrary spacing is not a valid option.

For each option score at minimum:

- semantic clarity;
- Stage 5 compatibility;
- no-hindsight safety;
- Live/pinned continuity;
- dense-session usability;
- long-gap usability;
- touch/mobile viability;
- accessibility viability;
- implementation determinism;
- aggregation compatibility;
- testability.

Stage 6.2 must recommend one v1 policy.
