# Stage 4.4 Review + Freeze Decision

## Review basis
Two submitted adversarial boards were reviewed as a single Stage 4.4 proof set:

- `S4.4_A_Adversarial_Navigation_Attacks_CANONICAL.png`
- `S4.4_B_Final_Navigation_Freeze_CANONICAL.png`

## Decision

**PASS — Stage 4 is ready to close.**

The proof set demonstrates that the navigation model survives all 20 attacks without requiring a new navigation primitive or reopening Stage 4.1–4.3.

### Why this closes Stage 4
- Lost-user recovery is available even after deep inspection away from Live Focus.
- Live Focus can move without hijacking manual inspection.
- Back / original inspection / Live Focus / World remain different recovery targets.
- Shared objects remain one identity across multiple contexts.
- Direct deep jumps retain or truthfully establish context.
- Peer Readings regain equal authority after deep inspection.
- Semantic depth remains understandable and reversible.
- Large world scale does not require canonical re-layout.
- Relation density stays secondary to places/orientation.
- Mobile/device change preserves geography and context lineage.
- Emerging Focus remains provisional/pre-geographic.
- Dormant/Reopened preserves one Thread identity/Home Anchor.
- Historical objects remain historically legitimate rather than retroactively false.
- Ordinary sharing remains distinct from a true Shared Anchor.
- Orientation does not depend solely on a minimap or text labels.
- Viewport center, density, Live Focus and inspected state do not become false importance signals.
- Rapid context switching does not require duplicate objects or corrupt return state.
- The experience remains a persistent semantic world rather than tabs/tree/dashboard/graph traversal.

## Non-blocking craft risks noted for later implementation
- very dense relation fields will need disciplined local disclosure/occlusion management;
- extremely narrow screens need especially strong but lightweight orientation cues;
- rapid multi-depth transitions will require careful motion choreography to preserve spatial continuity;
- large-scale rendering needs performance-aware progressive disclosure;
- first-use orientation affordances may need refinement without becoming tutorial chrome.

None of these are Stage 4 architectural blockers.

## Final freeze

**QANDEEL PAN + SEMANTIC ZOOM NAVIGATION v1 — FINAL FREEZE.**
