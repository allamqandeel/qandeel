# **Stage 5 — Timeline ↔ Map Synchronization**

Stage 5 must pair the frozen persistent spatial world with the frozen temporal model without turning QANDEEL into a timeline-first product.

### Upstream truths Stage 5 must preserve
- The Map is the persistent semantic world.
- The Timeline is a first-class **current-session temporal navigator**, not a replacement for the Map.
- Map navigation by itself does **not** move the temporal cursor.
- Explicit Timeline navigation may reorient the Map to the then-valid state/context.
- Three time concepts remain distinct:
  1. session position / conversational time,
  2. referenced temporal origin inside content,
  3. analysis availability / knowledge time.
- **K(t)** means what was legitimately knowable at time `t`; no hindsight leakage.
- Historical projection is the same persistent world filtered to then-valid knowledge/spatial facts, not a compact re-layout.
- Current Live Focus and manual Inspected Focus remain distinct from temporal cursor.
- Exact Return and inspection history from Stage 4 must survive Timeline interaction.

### Stage 5 central question
How can the user scrub/navigate time while keeping one stable spatial world and always understanding whether they are looking at:
- the live present,
- a historical session position,
- a referenced earlier event,
- or an analysis that became available later?

### Do not do at Stage 5 start
- do not design Replay as cinematic output;
- do not redesign Stage 3 visuals;
- do not reopen Stage 4 navigation;
- do not let Timeline become the primary information architecture;
- do not use later analysis as if it existed earlier.

Suggested first sub-stage:

**5.1 — Temporal State + Map Orientation Contract**

Freeze the precise interaction distinction among **Live Head, Temporal Cursor, Historical K(t), Inspected Focus, and Map Camera** before styling the Timeline.
