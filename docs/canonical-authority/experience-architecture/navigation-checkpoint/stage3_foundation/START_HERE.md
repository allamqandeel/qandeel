## North Star

> **QANDEEL Living Analysis Map — Conversation as an Unfolding Semantic World**

QANDEEL is not “chat + report.” A live conversation progressively becomes a persistent navigable world. Threads become stable places. Sessions leave inspectable footprints. Analytical and source objects remain epistemically distinguishable. Truthful relations may cross contexts. Deeper inspection reveals more meaning while preserving orientation in the same world.

## Canonical Stage 3 direction

> **QANDEEL Organic Living Field — Nebular World**

The world is a continuous, subtle cognitive-nebular environment: an abstract inner world, not literal land and not literal outer space. Established Threads are persistent organic places within it; each retains a stable perceptual locus. Neighborhood expression grows and adapts around that locus.

The nebula is **environment/substrate**, not a canonical object, relation, galaxy, constellation, or semantic graph.

## Mandatory semantic-zoom intent carried forward

> **Semantic Zoom ≠ optical magnification.**

Future navigation must support the lineage:

**World → Thread → Session → analytical/source material → source grounding**

At every deeper level, meaningful structure becomes clearer. A Session must unfold **from within the Thread field**, not open as a detached dashboard, modal, drawer, report page, or inspector panel.
