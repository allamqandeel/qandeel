## What this answers

> What may the current-session Timeline disclose, at what temporal state, at what semantic resolution, through which visual and nonvisual surfaces, without becoming a second analytical world or leaking future knowledge?

## The boundary, in one line

**The current-session Timeline discloses conversational time. It does not disclose analytical meaning.**

Readings, relations, evidence participation, questions and corrections are **MAP-ONLY**; confidence-over-time is **FORBIDDEN** as a Timeline class. Exactly one semantic-adjacent class is admitted — the *fact* of a focus transition, anonymously; its *identity* stays Map-only, because naming turns shape into a topical outline.

## The load-bearing rule

**`D-20` — post-`TC` non-metric reachability.** While `PINNED(TC)`, the region `TC < position ≤ LH` is a non-metric reachability affordance: its magnitude, subdivision, structure and density are independent of the Moments that followed. A proportional forward region is a graphical "of N" and defeats AT-04 without a single label. Metric resolution is licensed only by the user's own explicit forward Preview or commit, and only as far as they explicitly reached (`D-21`), and is withdrawn again on a backward commit (`D-22`).
