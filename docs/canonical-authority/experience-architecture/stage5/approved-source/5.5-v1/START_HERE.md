## The canonical correction, accepted

My Stage 5.4 formulation **`pos(e) = f(e)` is withdrawn** and is not used anywhere in this package. It was over-broad: it implied every canonical object owns an independent world coordinate, which is false. The binding invariant is **"projection changes never recompute canonical spatial commitments"** — `Home(e)` is assigned at establishment and constant thereafter, and Readings, Memories, Events, Relations, Evidence participations, Confidence states, Questions and ordinary contextual appearances gain **no** independent loci. Throughout this package, *"renders elsewhere"* therefore means **"is disclosed within another legitimate context"**, never "sits at another coordinate" — which is what makes D6 Case B and cross-context navigation statable at all.

## The rule everything else rests on

**Effective change is defined over the restorable tuple only:** `Φ = ⟨TM, TC, IF_ref, MC.region, MC.depth⟩`. Derived re-resolution of `IF_render` or `K(TC)`, and any movement of `LH`/`LF`, are **not** change. Without this single line, a live commit arriving while the user is pinned would re-resolve `IF_render` and masquerade as a state change — and D3 would be undecidable.

## Two apparent contradictions, examined and dismissed

- **D1 vs Stage 5.2's settle-time P3.** They answer different questions: D1 fixes *which referent the act means* (bound at activation); P3 fixes *whether the meant target is reachable* (evaluated at settle). Both are needed, or the command either chases a moving target or acts on a stale result.
- **D4 vs Stage 5.2 atomicity.** P3a is atomic *because* one act can resolve a unique locate; where ambiguity requires a human decision, that decision is a new act by definition. D4 states atomicity's **precondition** rather than amending it.

**No D-ruling contradicted a frozen contract, so no STOP arose.**

## The composite guarantee

**D1 + D5 together make it impossible for any return action to become a follow, or for a historical excursion to become unreachable.** Live may advance freely; the way back is always exactly one Back away. That is the property the whole stage exists to produce.
