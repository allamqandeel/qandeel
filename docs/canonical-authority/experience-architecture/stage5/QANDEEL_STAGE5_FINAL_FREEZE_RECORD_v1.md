# 3. AMB-01 — Final Frozen Resolution

`Go Live + Locate` uses:

## **POST-LIVE ONE-SHOT BINDING**

Canonical semantic order:

```text
P5 activation
→ temporal sub-effect establishes FOLLOW_LIVE
→ effective TC resolves to authoritative LH
→ at the logical post-live boundary:
     P5_LF* := then-current LF
→ locate once iff Locatable(P5_LF*, K(LH))
```

`P5_LF*` is:

- transient;
- act-local;
- not a member of `S`;
- not an RH checkpoint;
- not a temporal mode.

Later `LF` changes after binding do not retarget the in-flight action.

If the bound focus is Emerging/pre-geographic or otherwise unlocatable:

- the Live return remains valid;
- no fabricated camera landing occurs.

The entire P5 act remains one atomic RH transaction.

Stage 5.5 D1 remains distinct:

- **Return to Live Focus** binds `LF*` at activation.
- **Go Live + Locate** binds `P5_LF*` at the post-live boundary.

Neither becomes persistent follow.

## CLAR-03 — Pending contextual choice presentation is not a new state machine

A pending multi-locus contextual choice remains:

- transient;
- noncanonical;

Where the v2 proof describes an unrelated committed action as superseding/discarding the unmade choice, the frozen semantic requirement is only:

> the committed action executes from authoritative canonical state and the unmade choice carries no state/RH authority into it.

Exact dismissal wording, presentation lifetime and UI treatment of the outstanding choice remain later Experience work.
