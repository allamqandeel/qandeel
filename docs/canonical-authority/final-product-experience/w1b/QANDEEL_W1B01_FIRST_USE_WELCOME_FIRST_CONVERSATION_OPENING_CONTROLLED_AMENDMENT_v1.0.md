## 1. Why an amendment is needed

I-08A4 §14 froze a long, multi-paragraph first-use Welcome, opening with §13's short Product definition, and
§15 froze a First Conversation Opening with a list of examples. The Product Owner explicitly superseded both for
the production first-use surface with concise wording, and instructed that the historical record not be edited
in place.

## 2. Superseded copy (I-08A4, quoted exactly)

### §14 — Canonical First-Use Welcome

Arabic:

> **قنديل: نور يضيء لك نفسك، ويساعد أفضل ما فيك أن يظهر ويبقى.**
>
> أهلًا يا {display_name}.
>
> مع كل حديث بيننا سأعرفك أكثر، وأتذكر ما يهمك، وأربط ما تعيشه اليوم بما عرفته عنك من قبل؛ حتى لا يكون حديثنا مجرد لحظة عابرة، بل فهمًا ينمو مع الوقت.
>
> اهتمامي هنا بك أنت. سأصغي إليك، وأكون ناصحًا لك وأمينًا على ما تشاركه معي. لن أضعك في قالب، ولن أفرض عليك طريقًا؛ سأساعدك أن ترى الأمور من زوايا أوضح، وتفهم نفسك وحياتك وعلاقاتك بعمق أكبر.
>
> سأكون معك في ما يشغلك، وفي ما تطمح إليه، وفي اللحظات التي تتغير فيها. ومع الوقت، أساعدك أن تعرف أكثر ما يدفعك إلى الأمام، وما يحتاج منك إلى انتباه، وأن تجعل أفضل ما فيك يظهر… ثم يبقى.
>
> **ابدأ بما يشغلك الآن.**

English:

> **QANDEEL helps you see yourself more clearly, so the best in you can come forward — and stay.**
>
> Welcome, {display_name}.
>
> Every time we talk, I'll understand you a little better. I'll remember what matters to you and connect what you're living through today with what I've learned about you over time — so our conversations don't disappear as isolated moments, but build into understanding that grows with you.
>
> My attention here is on you. I'll listen carefully, offer honest guidance, and treat what you share with care. I won't put you in a box or push you toward a path. I'll help you look at things from clearer angles and understand yourself, your life, and your relationships more deeply.
>
> I'll be with you in what is on your mind, what you're working toward, and the moments when things change. Over time, I'll help you notice what moves you forward, what deserves your attention, and how to let the best in you come through — and stay.
>
> **Start with what's on your mind right now.**

### §15 — Canonical First Conversation Opening

Arabic:

> **أنا معك يا {display_name}.**
>
> ابدأ من أي شيء يشغلك الآن؛ فكرة، موقف، قرار، علاقة، أو حتى شيء لا تعرف كيف تصفه بعد. قل لي ما يدور في بالك، ونبدأ من هناك.

English:

> **I'm with you, {display_name}.**
>
> Start anywhere: a thought, a situation, a decision, a relationship, or even something you don't quite know how to put into words yet. Tell me what's on your mind, and we'll begin there.

## 3. The amended copy (Product Owner approved, verbatim)

### 3.1 First-use Welcome

Arabic:

> **أهلًا يا {display_name}.**
> **أنا قنديل. كل ما نتكلم أكثر، أفهمك أكثر وأتذكر ما يهمك، علشان أساعدك تشوف نفسك وحياتك بشكل أوضح.**
> **ابدأ بما يشغلك الآن.**

English:

> **Welcome, {display_name}.**
> **I'm QANDEEL. The more we talk, the better I understand you and remember what matters to you, so I can help you see yourself and your life more clearly.**
> **Start with what's on your mind.**

### 3.2 First Conversation Opening

Arabic — exact approved copy:

> **أنا معك يا {display_name}.**
> **ابدأ بما يشغلك الآن… حتى لو كان شيئًا لا تعرف كيف تصفه بعد. ونبدأ من هناك.**

English — semantic counterpart:

> **I'm with you, {display_name}.**
> **Start with what's on your mind… even if it's something you don't quite know how to describe yet. We'll begin there.**

## 4. What the amendment decides, and what it does not

| Question | Decision |
|---|---|
| The first-use flow (I-08A4 §12) | unchanged: relationship-first, no questionnaire, no second name question, dynamic `{display_name}`, then the real Conversation. The concise Welcome is realized as ONE coherent first-use surface: its second line («أنا قنديل. …» / "I'm QANDEEL. …") is where the Product is described to the reader, and its last line («ابدأ بما يشغلك الآن.» / "Start with what's on your mind.") is the start act, with no second action label |
| §13's short Product definition | its text is not changed, and it stays frozen where I-08A4 uses it. It is no longer the opening line of the first-use Welcome, because the approved Welcome does not contain it |
| The long manifesto | not shown on the production first-use surface, and not to be reintroduced there |
| The First Conversation Opening | stays a separate moment from the Welcome and from the normal opener (P4-C2 §5), with the copy of §3.2 |
| The normal new-conversation opener | unchanged — G1.1 §1 / P4-C4 `opener`: «اهلا يا {display_name} ... انا في انتظارك ... يلا نبدأ» / "Hi {display_name} ... I'm here, ready when you are ... let's begin". It needs no amendment |
| `{display_name}` | the account Name (P1 §2.3); never a fallback |

Everything else I-08A4 froze — Product hierarchy, Product Areas, navigation laws, canonical naming, §16's bilingual
realization requirement and the rest of §20 — stays exactly as I-08A4 and its later amendments have it.
